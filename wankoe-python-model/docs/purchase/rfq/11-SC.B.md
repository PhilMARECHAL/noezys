## 1. Scope of supply

One (1) double-deck fine dry screen, apertures 2.0 / 1.5 mm, making
BOTH zone-1.3 product cuts (grits 2/3.75, fines 0/1.5) — **the highest
RPN of the whole equipment park rides on its deck-1 panels (252)**; the
D6 grits envelope margin is only 0.8 pt. Complete with certified-
aperture quick-change panels (per-batch measured-aperture certificates
on delivery), TWO spare panel sets deck 1 + one set deck 2 (mandatory
priced line), designed-in aperture-gauging access, anti-pegging
provision (at least retrofit-ready), exciter/spares commonality per the
SC.A re-scope, commissioning supervision (mandatory priced line).

## 3. Witnessed tests

1. **D6 envelope acceptance (grits)**: deck-1 oversize sieve-verified
   **< 2 mm ≤ 15 % and > 4 mm ≤ 5 %** at the mode-G duty — the firm
   40 kt/y product.
2. **Fines acceptance**: deck-2 undersize meets the 0/1.5 spec AND
   redirect eligibility (≥ 95 % < 1.7 mm — naming rule: "redirect
   eligibility", never "AgLime spec", for zone-1.3 streams).
3. **Mode-F endurance**: 4 h at 50 t/h, stable sliver routing, no
   pegging collapse.
4. Aperture certificates checked panel-by-panel at delivery;
   gauging-access demonstration (monthly round within the stated time).
5. Exciter + stroke baselines (stroke drift must be measurable — cut
   sharpness degrades exactly where the D6 margin is thinnest).

## 4. Candidate bidders (vendor-candidates dossier)

**Rhewum (WA direct-excitation — best pick: per-batch aperture
certification is industry-standard ONLY on woven wire; direct cloth
excitation gives the sharpest d50 at 2.0 mm; static body decouples the
top-RPN machine from structural fatigue)**, Binder+Co (BIVITEC e+
double-deck — the anti-pegging alternate), Derrick (FTB double-deck,
Polyweb urethane), Spaleck (2-deck flip-flow), JOEST (OSCILLA
double-deck).

## 5. Machine-specific clauses

- The Rhewum guarantee must be quoted ON QUARTZ-BEARING FEED (5 %
  embedded, ~1.5–2 mm near-mesh heavy in mode F) — PU-mat alternates
  certify less tightly and must say so.
- Near-mesh statement: 44 t/h circulating regrind loop upstream at gap
  1.5 (error-hunt fix: 147 t/h was the gap-2.8 runaway, not the duty).
- The RC.1/RC.2 vendor gradation tests feed the same D6 margin — the
  three purchases are COUPLED: award sequencing must keep the
  sharpness guarantee consistent with the rolls' fixed n_comp/S_att.
- Ball-deck / anti-pegging demonstration; mode-F visual access.
- Cartridge/family re-scope per RFQ 10 G4 (stated honestly to bidders).
