## 3. 100 %-need verification (client order 2026-08-21) — honest verdict

**Dimensionally and in machine class: YES, both duties are covered.**
The two machines fit the zone-1.1 flowsheet (primary double-roll +
35/20 double-deck screen), clear every size/capacity/area floor, and
this quote resolves four earlier gaps: 2-deck screen (3-deck deviation
gone), declared weights/dimensions for both machines, a FIXED CIF, and
a LIVE price for the crusher (the expired-2025 caveat on OF-003 dies).

**Contractually: NO — not 100 %. Three gate-level items remain open:**

1. **The 20–60 mm setting window** (THE decisive RFQ 01 clarification):
   the spec sheet still prints "discharge opening set 40–800 mm".
   Purchase requires written confirmation of continuous adjustability
   across 20–60 mm at our duty (or the machine cannot make the 0/60
   product that protects the KFS window).
2. **No contractual product curves, no witnessed tests, no efficiency
   or certified-aperture guarantees** (golden rules 2/3; RFQ gates G3/
   G5): capacity figures are catalog ranges; the screen's supplied
   apertures (35/20) are not even stated.
3. **Payment terms collide with the Ndola frame**: 30 % down + balance
   before shipment = 100 % paid before the machines perform; our frame
   requires ≥ 20 % retained to the passed site performance test.

Also open: rain-duty (wet-feed) screening behavior, abrasivity basis
(5 % embedded quartz), spares/commissioning priced lines (absent here;
OF-002 had a 2-year spares part).
