# Segment 2 — GCC & Fine-Carbonate Plants Worldwide

World benchmark wave 1, NOEZYS due-diligence review, 2026-08-16. 16 real
plants + supporting references. Baseline = WANKOE zone 1.3 (fines 0/1.5
60 kt/y, UltraFin < 65 µm ~284 t/y quartz-free, dryer, small classifier).

## Cases (condensed — full sourcing in the register)

1. **Omya Orgon, FR — FULL.** Urgonian ~98–99 % white limestone; dry
   grinding + classification + FCC reactor line; food/pharma grades
   (acid-insolubles < 0.2–0.5 %). Lesson: certificate-of-analysis culture
   is the premium ticket.
2. **Omya Gummern, AT — PARTIAL.** Pure MARBLE flours; raw-material ladder:
   marble > pure limestone > chalk > eolianite. Treat our < 65 µm cut as
   "our marble".
3. **Omya Hustadmarmor, NO — FULL.** Wet GCC slurry at multi-Mt scale,
   dedicated tanker fleet (INFORMS-published logistics). Verdict: wet GCC
   is NOT for WANKOE (inland, small).
4. **Omya Verpol, VT, USA — PARTIAL.** Even Omya's flagship **purifies
   feed by flotation**. Impurity management is normal — ours (a 65 µm cut)
   is far cheaper.
5. **Imerys Marble Hill/Whitestone, GA — PARTIAL.** A GCC business living
   on CONSTRUCTION markets (roofing, joint compound, LVT; d97 20–75 µm) —
   the realistic first GCC market for eolianite fines.
6. **Sylacauga, AL (Imerys→Cimbar) — PARTIAL.** Ultra-white marble; food
   fortification from a natural deposit.
7. **Suomen Karbonaatti, Lappeenranta, FI — FULL.** JV: Nordkalk supplies
   beneficiated calcite, Omya mills and sells. **The ownership model to
   copy** (quarry + specialist).
8. **Nordkalk Pargas, FI — PARTIAL.** Nordkalk splits powder-grade vs
   GCC-grade BY DEPOSIT — WANKOE can split BY SIZE FRACTION.
9. **Calcit d.o.o., Stahovica, SI — FULL (equipment).** Ball mill closed
   with Hosokawa Turboplex 630/4 ATP, upgraded 2012 to **Alpine 1000 TTD**
   — the compact dry-GCC template (d97 ~5–45 µm family).
10. **ASCOM ACCM, Minya, EG — FULL (equipment).** Ball mill + Alpine 1000
    TTD on 99 %+ Egyptian white limestone; Egypt/Vietnam set the world
    commodity floor price — do not compete there.
11. **Reverté, Castellet/Albox, ES — PARTIAL.** Independent > 1 Mt/y on
    ≥ 98 % feed; PVC grades sold on low abrasion.
12. **Mikron'S, Niğde, TR — THIN.** Commodity micronized calcite.
13. **Niğtaş, Niğde, TR — PARTIAL.** ~2 Mt/y raw; nanometric + stearic
    COATED grades = where margins hide on cheap base mineral.
14. **Longcliffe, Brassington, UK — FULL.** A QUARRY company that moved up
    into powders (99.5 % rock; 2024: two new 14-t air-swept ball mills);
    feed & agri powders first, industrial fillers later — **the sequence
    to copy**.
15. **Provençale SA, Brignoles, FR — PARTIAL.** ~2nd European independent;
    **the natural offtaker/tolling partner** for a WANKOE quartz-free
    intermediate.
16. **Schaefer Kalk, Hahnstätten, DE — PARTIAL (contrast).** PCC — the
    engineered ultrafine ceiling; not a WANKOE route.

## The three strategic answers

1. **Is 284 t/y UltraFin worth a classifier?** Economically no,
   strategically yes — keep it as the **market-development pilot / sample
   factory** (sealed assayed lots to feed-premix, glass, putty buyers).
2. **Could a small mill + classifier make a real GCC line?** Yes — the
   most valuable latent asset: because the quartz liberates at 200 µm, a
   65 µm dynamic classification of the fines is a **purification step**
   (≥ ~99 % CaCO3 from a 95 % rock, cheaper than flotation). Phase A:
   scale the CUT (5–15 t/h dynamic classifier → 12–18 kt/y quartz-free
   intermediate). Phase B (offtake-gated): small air-swept ball mill for
   d97 40/20/10 µm construction fillers; coating later. Gates: brightness
   Ry ≥ 80–90, insolubles ≤ 1 %, Einlehner, Fe2O3 — **eolianites often
   fail on YELLOWNESS, not quartz: measure before believing**.
3. **Does 5 % quartz disqualify the fines as GCC feed?** As-is yes (specs
   < 0.5 % insolubles; 200 µm quartz = abrasion catastrophe); after the
   65 µm cut, no. Honest market for unclassified fines: feed-grade
   carbonate (≥ 95 % CaCO3 passes), agri, asphalt filler.

## Top-5 improvements (this segment)

1. Assay before steel (8 numbers: CaCO3, insolubles, SiO2, Fe2O3,
   brightness, Einlehner, PSD, heavy metals).
2. Monetize the 60 kt/y fines NOW at feed/agri grade.
3. Scale the 65 µm cut, not the mill (proper dynamic classifier).
4. Phase-gate a Calcit-style mini GCC circuit (offtake first).
5. Court a carbonate house (Provençale/Omya) before competing —
   Suomen Karbonaatti JV model.
