## 3. Gaps vs the NOEZYS RFQ frame (honesty section — what this offer is NOT yet)

1. **Payment terms COLLIDE with the Ndola-derived frame**: 30 % advance
   + 70 % BEFORE dispatch = 100 % paid before the machine even ships —
   the exact pattern the benchmark's Ndola failure anatomy forbids
   (our frame: effectiveness ≤ 10 %, ≥ 20 % retained until the passed
   site performance test, retention 12 months). This is gate-level:
   the terms must be renegotiated before any award.
2. **No product-curve guarantee**: "≤ 100 mm (90 %)" is a single point,
   not the contractual gradation curve at the 0/60 setting our RFQ 01
   makes the evaluation core (KFS-band preservation ≈ 12 kt/y of
   landfill per lost point). No witnessed-test offer, no value tables,
   no interpolation mode (golden rule 3 unmet).
3. **Screen apertures 40/20 vs specified 35/20** on the top deck —
   either a standard-panel proposal to be corrected to 35 mm (panel
   exchange is our own adjustability requirement, so likely trivial)
   or a misread of the duty; to be clarified. Wet/rain derating,
   efficiency guarantee and panel certification are not stated.
4. **FOB Shanghai excludes shipping**, erection, commissioning and
   spares — none of our mandatory priced lines (commissioning, FMECA
   spares kit) appears. Budget comparisons must add landing (+15–25 %
   [H] per the budget file).
5. **No wear/abrasivity basis**: the 5 % embedded-quartz context
   (RFQ §3) and tooth-segment life guarantee are absent.
6. 30-day validity (2026-09-13) is shorter than any full evaluation
   cycle with witnessed tests — an extension or re-issue will be
   needed.
