## 1. Scope of supply

One (1) double-deck dry screen, apertures 8 / 3.75 mm — the zone-1.3
recycle node (deck-1 oversize → RC.1, deck-2 oversize → RC.2, undersize
→ SC.B; chain stop if down). Complete with quick-change modular panels
+ one full spare set per deck (mandatory priced line), anti-pegging
option on the 3.75 mm deck, exciter condition-monitoring provision,
spare exciter cartridge SHARED with SC.B, aperture-gauging access,
commissioning supervision (mandatory priced line).

## 2. Guarantee points (pass/fail gates)

| # | Guarantee | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Deck areas | Deck 1 ≥ **6.0 m²**, deck 2 ≥ **7.0 m²** (worst mode F + 25 % [H]) — vendor bed-depth verification at ~92 t/h dry governs upward | Datasheet §4 |
| G2 | Efficiency on both decks | Vendor's binding efficiency guarantee (dry 8 / 3.75 mm cuts) — the loop balance has NO slack for misrouted recycle (RC.1 at 91.3 %, RC.2 at 100 % in mode F) | Datasheet §6 |
| G3 | Aperture ranges | Deck 1: 6.0–10.0 mm (step 0.5); deck 2: 3.5–4.5 mm (step 0.05) by panel exchange | Client rule 1 |
| G4 | Family commonality with SC.B | Exciter cartridge interchangeable with SC.B **as re-scoped**: if SC.B goes direct-excitation (Rhewum class — no cartridge exists), the interchange requirement transfers to the zone-1.2 BIVITEC family and this clause converts to a common-spares statement | Vendor-candidates finding 3, stated honestly |

## 3. Witnessed tests

1. **Cut-integrity acceptance**: three-stream sieve at duty — both cuts
   within the efficiency guarantee, loop mass balance closed.
2. **Loop stability tie-in**: 4 h mode-F run with stable RC.2
   circulating load (SC.A misrouting is the loop's main disturbance
   input).
3. Exciter + stroke baselines; panel quick-change demonstration;
   cartridge/spares demonstration per the G4 re-scope outcome.

## 5. Machine-specific clauses

- Feed basis: ~92 t/h dry recycle-loop feed (RC.1 + RC.2 products +
  fresh dried 6/20), abrasive dry limestone, 3 610 h/y — vendor sizing
  delivered as value tables with declared interpolation (golden
  rule 3).
- Monthly aperture gauging + loop-balance check is the maintenance
  plan: cut drift coarsens the RC.2 feed while RC.2 is at 100 % in
  mode F — gauging access must make the round fast.
- Anti-pegging media life on the 3.75 mm deck stated for quartz-bearing
  fines.
