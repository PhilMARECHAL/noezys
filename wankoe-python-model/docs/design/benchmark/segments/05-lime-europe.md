# Segment 5 — European Lime-Industry Stone-Preparation Plants

World benchmark wave 1, NOEZYS due-diligence review, 2026-08-16. 20 real
cases (2 permit/flowsheet-grade FULL). The closest mission analogues to the
WANKOE KFS chain.

## Cases (condensed)

1. **Lhoist Flandersbach/Wülfrath, DE — PARTIAL.** Europe's largest lime
   works (~7.5 Mt/y quarried; ~4 Mt/y sold UNburnt): stone prep is a
   product factory, not a kiln-feed filter; stone 1–250 mm across kiln
   types; radiometric belt weighing.
2. **Tarmac Tunstead, UK — FULL.** Kiln window **20/40**; **selective
   crushing + WASHING** to chemical-lime grade; historic fact: the wash
   fines are why a cement plant was built on site — cement still absorbs
   ~¼ of quarry output (under-sized stone). The "guaranteed bulk sink"
   model.
3. **Singleton Birch, Melton Ross, UK — PARTIAL.** Soft CHALK → 4 PFR
   kilns (400 kt/y lime); crushing/screening in-quarry; **Mogensen sizers
   ~40 years on sticky chalk**; £3M+ plant making **limestone fines for
   the Scunthorpe steelworks** — fines as a contracted product.
4. **Longcliffe, Brassington, UK — PARTIAL.** Scalping removes clay BEFORE
   crushing; dryer → mill → screen → air classify; graded powders 600 µm
   down to 2 µm — the zone-1.3 mission run as the whole business.
5. **Lhoist Réty (Chaux et Dolomies du Boulonnais), FR — FULL (permit).**
   9 shaft kilns, 1 950 t/d; stone arrives calibrated from the mega-quarry
   next door, and the lime plant STILL runs **2 guard-screening stations
   at 35 mm just upstream of the kilns** "to separate the fines present in
   the stone" — the screen-twice doctrine.
6. **Lhoist Sorcy, FR — PARTIAL.** ~1 Mt/y stone burned in 2 kilns fed by
   **THREE kiln-stone calibers: 10/30, 30/50, 50/100 mm** — nearly the
   whole curve above 10 mm is burned. The single most valuable structural
   idea for the 24.9 % KFS yield.
7. **Carmeuse Moha, BE — PARTIAL.** New 1 000 t/h stone line WITH
   **logwashers + WWTP** inside the primary plant — washing as the
   rain-country norm.
8. **Carmeuse Aisemont, BE — PARTIAL.** **€16.5 M, 450 kt/y limestone
   filler mill — the largest in Benelux** — built expressly to valorize
   below-kiln-grade stone (vs our 13.8 kt/y landfill).
9. Lhoist Jemelle, BE — THIN. Crusher+kiln+mill triad standard.
10. **Schaefer Kalk Hahnstätten, DE — PARTIAL.** Stone "crushed, sieved,
    WASHED"; 14 kilns of different duties absorb several calibers; fines
    escape route = PCC.
11. Fels Oberharz, DE — PARTIAL. 7 kilns (3 annular-shaft + 2 PFR) absorb
    multiple fractions; rail logistics.
12. **Fels Saal an der Donau, DE — FULL.** **Sandvik SR roller screen**
    ahead of the kiln: 0/60 wet sticky limestone cut at 15 mm, 100 t/h,
    non-clogging discs, "constant performance even in winter" — kiln takes
    15/60. THE wet-screening exemplar.
13. **Kalkfabrik Netstal, CH — FULL.** Kiln stone made with **two
    double-roller crushers (minimum fines generation)**, then screened +
    WASHED into 15/40 + 40/100, both burned in equal parts (Maerz PFR
    200 t/d). Roll-crush-for-kiln-stone doctrine at ~30 t/h washing scale.
14. Nordkalk Pargas, FI — THIN. Electrified crushing.
15. **Nordkalk Storugns/Klinthagen, SE — PARTIAL.** Primary at the quarry,
    3.2 km conveyor; kiln stone doubles as merchant steel-flux export.
16. **Verdalskalk/NorFraKalk, NO — PARTIAL.** One quarry feeds multiple
    kiln types at multiple sites — the size curve sold across windows;
    rotary kilns take the finer stone.
17. SMA Mineral Rättvik/Jutjärn, SE — THIN.
18. **Lhoist Faxe, DK — PARTIAL.** Soft bryozoan limestone; **XRT sorter
    removes flint** from marginal benches (works wet/dusty), doubling
    quarry life; reject sold as aggregate. Sensor sorting as the embedded-
    contaminant answer.
19. Trzuskawica (CRH), PL — THIN. ~4 Mt/y stone department + 12 kilns.
20. Chaux de Boran, FR — verified exists (Lhoist France), THIN.

Bonus (Maerz/ZKG kiln windows actually burned): Fassa Ceraino 40/80 and
30/60+40/80; Kalcit Slivnitsa 40/80; Gangyuo Kucevo 60/110.

## Segment synthesis — what European lime plants do differently

1. **Kiln windows are wider and often multiple**: 20/40, 30/60, 40/80,
   60/110, 15/60, 15/40+40/100 dual, 10/30+30/50+50/100 triple. **A single
   narrow 20/35 window is the outlier and is the root of the 24.9 %
   yield.**
2. **Water is a tool, not a threat**: washing and wet-capable roller
   screens are the norm in our rain profile; nobody plans 25 % downtime.
3. **Fines never go to landfill**: filler mills, cement works, steel flux,
   AgLime, PCC.
4. **Fines are PREVENTED**: roll/sizer crushing for kiln stone (Netstal),
   scalping at the face (Longcliffe/Klinthagen), guard screens at the kiln
   bunker (Réty 35 mm, Saal 15 mm).
5. **The stone curve is sold across several customers/kiln types** —
   stone prep run as a mine-to-market business.

## Top-5 improvements (this segment, ranked)

1. **Attack the KFS window** (evidence: PFR kilns burn 15/40–40/80; Sorcy
   three calibers; Netstal dual feed): widened 20/40–20/50 or a second
   caliber (35/60) → yield 24.9 → 35–50 %.
2. **Fines valorization line instead of landfill** (Aisemont/Tunstead/
   Singleton Birch): milled filler, cement raw, steel/sinter flux, FGD.
3. **Roller (disc) screens for wet-season cuts** (Fels Saal proof) on the
   35/20 double-deck and 6 mm cut.
4. **Roll/sizer secondary instead of impactor in the KFS loop** (Netstal
   doctrine — our impactor is a fines generator feeding our landfill).
5. **Small wash step + 20 mm guard screen at KFS load-out** (Moha/Netstal
   washing; Réty screen-twice doctrine — soft stone degrades in stock).

Watchlist: Faxe XRT if WANKOE geology shows discrete silica lenses;
Longcliffe-style graded-powder ladder above UltraFin.
