# Segment 6 — Soft / Coastal / Eolianite Limestone & Chalk Operations

World benchmark wave 1, NOEZYS due-diligence review, 2026-08-16. 20 real
operations incl. 3 honest verified-negatives. Our ROCK analogues.

## Group 1 — Eolianite / calcarenite

1. **Cockburn Cement Munster, WA — FULL.** (see segment 1) — NO crushing
   at all; wet shell sand → drained stockpiles → long rotary kilns that
   dry + calcine. The "match the kiln to the rock" proof at >1 Mt/y lime.
2. **WA Limestone Group, Tamala quarries, Perth — PARTIAL.** Rip-and-push
   on our twin rock; >30 products; **soft crushed limestone sold as
   HYDRAULICALLY-BINDING ROADBASE** (recements after compaction) — Perth's
   whole industry monetizes exactly what we landfill.
3. **Meteor Stone / Italia Stone / Stoneridge, Nowergup WA — PARTIAL.**
   Quarry-SAWN natural blocks + **reconstituted blocks = crushed fines +
   cement, vibro-cast** — two premium outlets for weak-limestone material.
4. **Bruhn/Marte, Mount Gambier SA — PARTIAL.** Coquina cut damp with
   circular saws; **case-hardens as it dries** — argues for air-curing
   kiln stone under roof before handling.
5. **Minya cluster, Egypt — PARTIAL.** World's biggest soft-limestone
   SAWING district (no blasting anywhere); block waste feeds GCC plants;
   desert dryness is what makes their fines processing cheap — our real
   cost driver is water, not comminution.
6. **Malta globigerina "franka" quarries — FULL.** UCS ~15–25 = closest
   published match; ~60 quarries, rotary-saw blocks, NO crushing sector;
   sawing fines sold for mortar; selective extraction vs bad horizons.
7. **Bermuda/Bahamas — THIN, largely verified-negative** for industrial
   crushing (historic sawing only; Ocean Cay ended).
8. **Israel kurkar — THIN, verified-negative**: quartz-rich eolianite
   quarrying ABANDONED — judged not worth processing.
9. **Gulf caprock — THIN + one negative + one positive**: Qatar imports
   gabbro rather than process weak Simsima limestone (market verdict:
   soft coastal limestone ≠ premium aggregate); Saudi Lime Industries
   (97–99 % CaCO3, desert); **Ma'aden: Wirtgen 2500 SM at 800 t/h cutting
   25 MPa phosphate + 80 MPa limestone selectively since 2012**.

## Group 2 — Chalk industry (softest carbonate, wettest culture)

10. **Cemex Kensworth → Rugby, UK — FULL.** Chalk at ~20 %+ moisture:
    crush → WASHMILL to slurry → **92 km pipeline** → drier-crusher at the
    cement plant. "Go fully wet" philosophy; the drier-crusher (flash
    dry+mill in one machine) is the transferable piece.
11. **Holcim Lägerdorf, DE — FULL.** Chalk at 40 % moisture;
    **chamber filter presses dewater 40 → 20 % WITHOUT FUEL** before the
    thermal step; next phase = floating dredge in a mining lake.
    Mechanical dewatering before thermal drying — directly transplantable.
12. **Singleton Birch, UK — FULL** (see segment 5) — the closest
    full-chain analogue: soft chalk → PFR kilns + aglime + steel fines,
    Mogensen sizers 40 years.
13. **Lhoist Faxe, DK — PARTIAL** (see segment 5) — XRT flint sorting.
14. **Dankalk, Aggersund, DK — PARTIAL.** Chalk crushed → DRIED →
    pulverized → sieved into feed chalk/aglime/filler; **dryer waste heat
    exported as district heating to Løgstør** — heat integration as
    revenue.
15. **VK Dammann, Söhlde, DE — FULL (company docs).** Drying drums →
    hammer mills → sieves → air classifiers; feed chalk + fertilizer lime
    + dust-free granulate — **the exact WANKOE zone-1.3 flowsheet running
    for decades on the stickiest carbonate there is.**
16. Omya Omey, FR — THIN. Champagne chalk GCC.

## Group 3 — Surface miners & mass mining

17. **IFCEM Ivano-Frankivsk, UA — FULL (Wirtgen case).** Limestone UCS
    20–70 MPa: **3 surface miners (2200/2500 SM) cut, crush AND load in
    one pass, 7 000 t/d, no blasting, no primary crusher** — the 2200 SM
    class is exactly WANKOE's 250 t/h scale and would replace ripper +
    CR.5009 while mining quartz-rich benches selectively.
18. **Ma'aden, KSA — PARTIAL.** 2500 SM, 800 t/h, selective cutting.
19. **Titan America Pennsuco, FL — PARTIAL/FULL.** Miami oolite (soft
    porous coastal): underwater blast + dragline + **drainage windrows
    before crushing** — dewatering-by-time at industrial scale.
20. Pacific Aggregate, Oahu — THIN. Coral aggregate exists commercially.

## Segment synthesis

1. **Nobody with rock this soft fights for lump the way WANKOE does** —
   three cultures: SAW it (Malta/Gambier/Minya/Perth blocks), GO FULLY WET
   (Kensworth slurry, Lägerdorf presses, Cockburn wet kiln feed), or
   DRY-ROUTE FINES (Söhlde/Dankalk/Minya GCC) with drying immediately
   ahead of milling and heat integration. WANKOE sits between (b) and (c)
   without the compensating benefits of either.
2. **Screening moist soft carbonate is a solved problem** (Mogensen /
   flip-flow / sizer-screens, 40-year references).
3. **Surface miners are proven at exactly our strength and scale.**
4. **The 0/20 "excess" is a product everywhere else** (roadbase,
   reconstituted blocks, pipe bedding, kiln feed) — but NEVER premium
   concrete aggregate (Qatar's verdict).
5. **Moisture is managed with time, mechanics and roofs before fuel**
   (windrows, filter presses, covered storage, waste-heat recovery);
   fuel-fired drying is the LAST resort on the MINIMUM stream.

## Top-5 improvements (this segment)

1. **Trial a Wirtgen 2200 SM-class surface miner** to replace ripper +
   CR.5009 (cuts, crushes to ~0/60, loads, mines selectively).
2. Re-screen the wet circuit on sizer/flip-flow technology (recover the
   0.75 derate; push the wet cut-point down).
3. **Mechanically dewater + roof stockpiles before thermally drying**
   (filter press / drainage windrows / covered surge; Dankalk waste-heat
   recovery) — attack the paraffin bill.
4. Turn the 0/20 landfill stream into product: hydraulic roadbase (Perth),
   reconstituted blocks, or moist-fines kiln feed (Cockburn model).
5. XRT sensor sorting on the kiln-stone stream if geology shows discrete
   silica lenses (Faxe model).
