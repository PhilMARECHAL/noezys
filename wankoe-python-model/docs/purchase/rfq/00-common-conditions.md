## 1. Contractual frame (lessons of record — Ndola failure anatomy, benchmark segment 11)

1. **Milestone payments against witnessed performance tests only.** No
   payment tranche is released on delivery alone; the schedule is:
   effectiveness (≤10 %) → FAT passed (≤30 %) → delivery (≤20 %) →
   commissioning complete (≤20 %) → PERFORMANCE TEST PASSED on WANKOE
   material at site (≥20 % retained).
2. **Retention and guarantees.** 10 % retention (bank guarantee
   acceptable) released 12 months after the passed performance test;
   advance-payment bond for any pre-payment; parent-company guarantee
   where the bidder is a subsidiary.
3. **Obligations survive to FULL PERFORMANCE, not delivery.** The
   guarantee points of each RFQ remain enforceable through the
   performance-test period; liquidated damages apply per unmet guarantee
   point (rates in the commercial annex at sending time).
4. **Supplier solvency verified before award** (financial statements,
   references contacted, escrow of critical drawings/software).
5. **Commissioning is part of the scope**: vendor supervision until the
   performance test passes; a named commissioning budget line is
   MANDATORY in every offer (unpriced commissioning = non-conforming).
6. **Witnessed tests on WANKOE material.** Each RFQ lists its tests;
   client (or its engineer) attends; value tables with declared
   interpolation are contract deliverables (project golden rules 2/3).
7. **Spares.** Every offer prices the datasheet's FMECA-derived initial
   spares kit as a separate mandatory line, with lead times.
8. **Data/QC deliverables**: as-built drawings, settings ranges vs the
   datasheet tables, maintenance plan aligned to the project FMECA,
   certificates (material, aperture, balancing) as listed per RFQ.

## 2. Evaluation method (common grid)

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Compliance with the guarantee points (pass/fail gates first) | gate |
| Technical quality vs the datasheet duty (margins, references on comparable rock) | 30 % |
| Total cost of ownership 10 y (price + energy + wear parts + spares) | 30 % |
| Guarantee strength (test protocol accepted, LD terms, wear-life commitments) | 20 % |
| Delivery + commissioning schedule credibility | 10 % |
| After-sales presence and references reachable | 10 % |

Non-conforming on any pass/fail gate = eliminated regardless of price.
Chinese and Western bidders are evaluated on the SAME grid; witnessed
tests on client material are the equalizer for documentation asymmetry
(benchmark finding, CR.5009 dossier §5).

## 3. Material context given to every bidder

Rock: soft porous aeolian limestone, UCS 15–30 MPa (ref 20), moisture
7–12 % (outdoor stocks, 25 % rain time), **5 % silica as well-rounded
~200 µm quartz grains embedded in the mass** (abrasivity annex:
docs/design/abrasivity/ — LCPC ABR ~310 g/t [250–420] simulated [H];
"soft rock ≠ low wear"; regime map per machine class). All [H] values
are declared as hypotheses; NO external test campaign has been launched
(client decision 2026-08-16) — bidders must state which guarantees they
can give on the declared data and which require their own material
tests (sample shipments to be arranged at RFQ stage).
