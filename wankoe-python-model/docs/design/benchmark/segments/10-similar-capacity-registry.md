## What the peer class shows (beyond waves 1-2)

1. **The class norm is our shape**: own quarry + crushing/screening +
   kilns + an AGGREGATES/PRODUCTS SIDE-STREAM absorbing the non-kiln
   fractions (Istein 150+170, Palagiano 2/3+1/3, Walhalla 200+200+350,
   Faulkner lime+carbonate). Nobody in the class runs kilns without a
   stone-products arm — WANKOE's multi-product instinct is the norm, its
   landfill line is not.
2. **Audited-actual benchmarks now exist in the file**: Unicalce EMAS
   declarations (stone actuals by year), US Lime SEC 10-K (capacity,
   reserves, margins), Ohio EPA t/h permit basis (54.5 t/h ≈ our 250 t/h
   at their utilization) — the material for a numbers-level wave-4 DD.
3. **Alternative dryer/kiln fuels in-class**: cork dust (Calcidrata),
   biomass kilns (Unicalce Narni), carbide gas (out-of-class Mundra) —
   fuel optionality is normal at our size.
4. **Wet-season peers operate**: Ndola/Limeco (Zambian rains), Homa
   (bimodal Kenya), Te Kuiti (Waikato), Tapah (year-round tropical),
   Sinar Asia Fortuna (wet-season constraint documented) — none plans
   25 % downtime.
5. **Wave-4 deep-DD shortlist (client arbitration)**: Unicalce Palagiano
   (structural twin, audited), Kalkwerk Istein (data-complete), Calcidrata
   (ownership model), Limestone Resources Ndola (climate + revival),
   Carmeuse Winchester / US Lime St. Clair (cost benchmarks), Graymont
   Faulkner (dual-product twin), Sigma Minerals (growth case), Chichibu
   (Asia verified), Graymont Te Kuiti (structure + wet climate).
