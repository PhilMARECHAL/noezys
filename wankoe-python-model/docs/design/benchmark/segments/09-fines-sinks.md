# Segment 9 (Wave 2) — Industrial Sinks for Limestone Fines / 0-20

World benchmark wave 2, NOEZYS due-diligence review, 2026-08-16. 15
documented cases WITH acceptance specs; pass/fail verdicts against WANKOE
0/20 (95 % CaCO3, ~5 % embedded 200 µm quartz, 7–12 % moisture, 13.8 kt/y
landfilled) and 0/1.5 fines (60 kt/y). Depth: 8 FULL, 7 PARTIAL, 0 THIN.

## Cases and specs (condensed)

1. **Polish FGD sorbent market — FULL.** Bełchatów ~1.5 Mt/y etc.;
   suppliers Lhoist Polska, Czatkowice (> 96 %), Nordkalk Wolica. Flour
   spec: CaCO3 ≥ 94 %, Fe2O3 < 0.4 %, < 100–120 µm, high reactivity.
   → WANKOE flour route: EFFECTIVE FAIL (insolubles + mill wear vs 96–98 %
   competitors).
2. **India CEA wet-FGD standard spec — FULL.** Limestone received −25 mm,
   ground on site; design SiO2 2.1–4.5 %. → 0/20 as CRUSHED STONE:
   CONDITIONAL PASS (5 % just above design ceiling; moisture OK).
3. **Mulzer "scrubber stone", Ohio Valley — PARTIAL.** Utilities buy
   crushed stone barge-shipped; quartz negotiable where gypsum is
   landfilled.
4. **Tarmac Tunstead cement kiln — FULL (the model).** The kiln was BUILT
   to eat washing fines; 95 % CaCO3 fines = raw-meal "sweetener".
   → PASS both streams (200 µm quartz = burnability discount, not
   rejection; kilns dry routinely).
5. NLMK→Eurocement 520 kt/y × 30 y limestone contract — PARTIAL (scale
   proof).
6. **Singleton Birch → Scunthorpe sinter flux — FULL.** −3.35 mm chalk
   fines with ~2 % flint; sinter spec CaCO3 ≥ 90 %, SiO2+Al2O3 ≤ 6 %,
   band ~0.2–3.35 mm. → PASS (0/1.5 near-spec; 0/20 one crush/screen).
   BOF steelmaking flux (SiO2 ≤ 1.5 %): FAIL.
7. **Carmeuse Aisemont filler 450 kt/y — FULL.** EN 13043: ~70–100 %
   < 63 µm, methylene-blue cleanliness, water ≤ ~1 %. NO quartz bar, NO
   CaCO3 floor. → quartz PASSES; moisture FAILS as-is (dry + mill
   needed).
8. LKAB Stockton filler line — PARTIAL. 9. BS 8500:2023 limestone fines
   in concrete (~20 % third component) — PARTIAL.
10. **Main Roads WA Spec 501 roadbase — FULL.** CaCO3 ≥ 60 % floor;
    self-cementing dune limestone standard in Perth. → chemistry trivial
    PASS; 0/20 = fines fraction of a blended hydraulic roadbase; local
    spec acceptance is the work.
11. **VK Dammann "Granukal" datasheet — FULL.** Commercial granulated
    aglime with **SiO2 typical 5 %** on its own datasheet; granules
    > 97 % < 3.15 mm. → PASS; the direct analog for milled+granulated
    WANKOE fines.
12. LKAB Calcifert granulated lime — PARTIAL (binder pan granulation;
    tolling availability unverified — direct outreach needed).
13. Omya Calciprill (Bathurst NSW, Waikato NZ plants) — PARTIAL.
    Granulation absorbs 10–20 kt/y realistically, not 60.
14. WV AMD stream dosing — PARTIAL. 0/1.5 technically near-perfect;
    volumes kt-scale at best. Niche.
15. **MSHA coal-mine rock dust 30 CFR 75.2 — FULL SPEC, HONEST FAIL.**
    ≤ 4 % total silica (< our 5 %) + no-caking clause (vs our moisture).

## The ranked fines ladder for WANKOE

1. **Cement raw material** (Tunstead model) — the only sink that swallows
   BOTH streams whole; gated by haul distance only. Action: chemistry
   dossier + trial delivery.
2. **Sinter flux** to an integrated steelworks — quartz passes; exact
   chalk-with-flint precedent. Gated by distance to a sinter plant.
3. **FGD crushed stone** (NOT flour) to plants milling on site —
   plant-by-plant conditional; avoid wallboard-gypsum plants.
4. **Granulated aglime** — highest €/t for 0/1.5; 5 % SiO2 commercially
   normal (Granukal); micronize + granulate capex or tolling; 10–20 kt/y.
5. **EN 13043 / BS 8500 filler** — blocked only by drying + fine milling;
   partner with an existing filler mill; 20–50 kt/y plausible.
6. **Roadbase/sub-base** — landfill-avoidance backstop for 0/20; low
   value, local spec work.
7. AMD neutralization — technically perfect, commercially marginal.

**Honest fails**: MSHA rock dust (silica 4 % cap); BOF flux (SiO2 1.5 %);
Polish-type FGD flour; any dry powder market without a drying step.
