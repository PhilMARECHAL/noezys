## 3. Witnessed tests

1. **Mode-F guarantee test**: 25 t/h per unit sustained 2 h at gap
   1.5 mm on WANKOE material — gap held within 0.1 mm, no overload
   release, product P80 consistent with 2.6 mm.
2. **Gradation acceptance** = the G4 test; coefficients re-fitted
   data-first and the two-mode plan replayed by the engine before
   acceptance.
3. **Gap changeover demonstration** 3.4 → 1.5 → 3.4, both units.
4. **Loop stability**: 4 h mode-F run, no runaway circulating load;
   fines 0/1.5 spec + redirect eligibility (≥ 95 % < 1.7 mm) and grits
   D6 at SC.B.
5. Per-unit baselines; twin comparative trend configured.
