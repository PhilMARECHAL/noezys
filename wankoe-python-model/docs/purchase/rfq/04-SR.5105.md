## 1. Scope of supply

One (1) single-deck screen, aperture 6 mm, wet reclaim screening of the
0/20 stockpile (FeedLime 6/20 oversize / 0/6 undersize to the AgLime
loop) — the single point of zone 1.2. Complete with quick-change
modular panels + one full spare set (mandatory priced line),
anti-blinding execution for the wet 6 mm cut (no spray water
available), exciter condition-monitoring provision, aperture-gauging
access, commissioning supervision (mandatory priced line).

## 3. Witnessed tests

1. **FeedLime 6/20 cut acceptance**: oversize PSD spot check at
   100 t/h wet.
2. **AgLime channel protection**: undersize 0/6 verified free of
   aperture-wear oversize.
3. **Blinding endurance**: 4 h sustained wet run without
   effective-open-area collapse.
4. Exciter vibration + stroke baselines; panel quick-change
   demonstration; family-cartridge interchange demonstration.

## 4. Candidate bidders (vendor-candidates dossier)

**Binder+Co (BIVITEC ~1.3×4.0 m flip-flow — best pick; one BIVITEC
family across SR.5105/5111/5115 closes the shared-cartridge requirement
structurally)**, IFE (TRISOMAT), Hein Lehmann (LIWELL LF), Spaleck
(flip-flow), Metso (Compact CVB1540 with self-cleaning media —
conventional fallback).

## 5. Machine-specific clauses

- The wet 6 mm cut is the most blinding-prone duty of zone 1.2: the
  vendor's sizing statement must declare its moisture assumption and
  its mat acceleration / self-cleaning mechanism.
- Family offer: a single quotation covering SR.5105 + SR.5111 + SR.5115
  with one common exciter/drive cartridge spare is REQUESTED alongside
  any per-machine offer.
- Mats/panels stated for quartz-bearing limestone (5 % embedded,
  §3 common conditions).
