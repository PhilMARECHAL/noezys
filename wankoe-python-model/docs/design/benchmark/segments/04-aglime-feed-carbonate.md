# Segment 4 — Agricultural Lime & Feed-Grade Carbonate Producers

World benchmark wave 1, NOEZYS due-diligence review, 2026-08-16. 18 real
producers/programs + the EU regulatory frame. WANKOE's own markets.

## Regulatory reality check — the 5 % quartz

**EU Reg. 68/2013 entry 11.1.1 (calcium carbonate/limestone): mandatory
"ash insoluble in HCl" declaration if > 5 %.** WANKOE sits EXACTLY on the
trigger: legal below, label-flagged above — and the MARKET is tougher
(competitors headline 96.8–99 % CaCO3 / "low acid-insolubles"). Poultry
science validates our granulometry exactly (2–4 mm grit vs 0.1–0.4 mm meal
in layer studies).

## Segment synthesis

1. **Nobody dries aglime** (moist is normal; Germany moistens on purpose;
   cage mills and flip-flow screens handle wet feed; damp tiers sold on
   dry-basis NV).
2. **Everybody dries feed lime — then screens into narrow fractions and
   BLENDS to order.** Fire-dry → fraction-screen → blend is the universal
   feed flowsheet; **precision roll gaps appear NOWHERE in the segment.**
3. **Feed carbonate is a purity business** (96–99 % CaCO3, GMP+/FEMAS
   certification are entry tickets).
4. **Fines are a product** (granulation: Calcifert/AtriGran/Granukal;
   stall lime: Barn-Dri/KSM).
5. **Quality is certified, not just specified** (state RNV/ENM programs,
   DAFM licensing, GMP+/FEMAS).

## Top-5 improvements (this segment)

1. De-risk the 5 % quartz commercially: per-bench insolubles assay +
   blending to hold < 5 % with margin; target ≤ 3 %; get GMP+/FEMAS.
2. **Rethink the 1.5 mm precision-gap concept**: the industry serves the
   same markets with dry → fraction screening → blending (0.1–0.3 /
   0.3–0.6 / 0.6–1.0 / 1.6–2.0 + 2/4) — more spec-flexible, wear-tolerant.
3. Kill the dry-season-only AgLime constraint: flip-flow 1.7 mm at 7–12 %
   moisture and/or a 4-row cage mill open-circuit (both proven on aglime).
4. Add a "damp AgLime" second tier sold on dry-basis NV (zero capex).
5. Valorize the landfill stream: granulated lime + stall/cubicle lime.
