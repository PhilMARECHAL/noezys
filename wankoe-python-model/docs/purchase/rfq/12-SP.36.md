## 1. Scope of supply

One (1) FULL-STREAM dynamic air classifier (Ventoplex C25V reference
class, INTERNAL recirculation fan) ingesting the WHOLE 0/1.5 fines
stream — **13.6 t/h (mode G) to 23.3 t/h (mode F)** — and cutting at
d50c 65 µm. Complete with ceramic/hard-faced classifier wheel and vanes
(MANDATORY — silica study REV B), circuit vent bag filter (ePTFE,
≤ 5 mg/Nm³, dust labeled "may contain RCS pending XRD"), Q_air /
rotor-speed instrumentation for cut verification, spare wheel wear set
(mandatory priced line), commissioning supervision (mandatory priced
line).

Architecture consequences already ruled by the client: the external
420 m³/h fan (PD-3) is CANCELLED; the CL.38 product cyclone is
WITHDRAWN — neither appears in this RFQ.

## 3. HONESTY CEILING (stated to every bidder, client-accepted)

The natural sub-65 µm content of the fines is **0.60–0.66 %** (engine
PSD, modes G/F): the machine harvests **at most ~430 t/y of UltraFin**
from classification. The client knows this and buys the machine for:
capture of ALL natural ultrafines + full-stream de-dusting of the fines
product + readiness for a future mill circuit. A bidder claiming
kilotonne UltraFin yields from this feed is non-conforming on honesty
grounds. (Since 2026-08-16 the dedusting strategy also routes ALL
zone-1.3 filter dust to sellable UltraFin — ~6 700 t/y at current
defaults — but that stream comes from the FILTERS, not this machine;
it does not change this machine's duty.)

## 4. Witnessed tests

1. **Cut acceptance**: d50c 65 µm verified by laser/sieve sizing of
   BOTH products at the mode-F duty; efficiency ≥ guarantee. This test
   simultaneously CERTIFIES the cut (Phi(<cut) is unmeasured — standing
   alert closed by this test).
2. **Adjustability**: cut swept across 45–150 µm by the operating
   lever, verified at two extra points.
3. **Full-stream endurance**: 4 h at 23.3 t/h, stable internal air
   circuit, vent filter emission ≤ 5 mg/Nm³.
4. Vibration baselines; Q_air/rotor instrumentation calibrated against
   the acceptance operating points.

- The vent bag filter is IN this scope (ePTFE, RCS-scoped); its
  catch joins the sellable-UltraFin dedusting circuit (client strategy
  2026-08-16) — filter-catch composition is certified by the same XRD
  that scopes RCS.
- Internals inspectable annually without full dismantling.
- Cut-verification instrument in the circuit (the 6-monthly check
  against photo references).
- Vendor states its air-circuit tail-dust loading so the residual
  sub-4 µm design gap stays closed coherently with the vent filter.
