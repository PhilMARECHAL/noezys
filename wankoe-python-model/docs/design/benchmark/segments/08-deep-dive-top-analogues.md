# Segment 8 (Wave 2) — Deep-Dive Due Diligence: the Five Closest Analogues

World benchmark wave 2, NOEZYS due-diligence review, 2026-08-16. Full-depth
DD (history, ownership, permits, incidents, economics) on the five closest
WANKOE analogues. Unverifiable items explicitly flagged.

## 1. Singleton Birch, Melton Ross, UK — the durability model

Est. 1815 (CH no. 00009433); UK's largest independent lime producer;
FY2023 EBITDA £7 M (+47 %), ~150 staff. **Acquired Sept 2022 by
Mississippi Lime (HBM)** — brand/management retained. Mogensen 5-deck
sizer installed ~40 y ago because a conventional screen would not FIT —
compact multi-deck sizers solved a footprint + sticky-chalk problem for
four decades. British Steel Scunthorpe = stated long-term flux customer
(terms not public — flagged). Energy diversification EARLY: 1.5 MW
anaerobic digestion 2014 (~40 % of site power) → PlanET Biogas stake 2018
→ Origen zero-carbon lime trial 2020 → Centrica hydrogen (HAR2, ~20 % of
kiln energy, commissioning 2028). EA permit EPR/BL8805IZ incl. a granted
BAT derogation (V011, 2021); no major enforcement found (absence ≠ proof).
**Teaches**: anchor customer + vertical energy integration + staying
acquirable = 200-year survival of a soft-carbonate single-quarry site.

## 2. Cockburn Cement, Munster + Dongara, WA — the social-licence ledger

Shellsand dredging since 1972 under a 1971 State Agreement; EPA staged
approvals (Report 1033/2001; Ministerial Statement 599/2002: Stage 1
2002-2014, Stage 2 2015-2034, 783 ha incl. 168.5 ha seagrass — the
central controversy); 1997 royalty clawback; kiln 6 dust: forced
**A$23 M bag filter (2012)** + first mandatory feed-stop condition;
**A$290 000 fine (2023)** for odour; clinker ceased 2015 (lime kilns 5-6
remain, > 1 Mt/y); 2026 licence rewrite explicitly permits limestone /
lime sand / shell sand feed + pilot RTO by Aug 2026. Dongara (~A$40 M,
1998) = the LAND-WON hedge. CRH+Barro took Adbri (A$2.1 bn, 2024).
Live public emissions feeds = condition of social licence.
**Teaches**: a contested resource costs 50 years of staged consents,
retrofits and fines; hold a second politically clean deposit; wet feed
direct to kiln works but exports the moisture problem to the stack and
the neighbours — enclose and publish monitoring from day one.

## 3. Kalkfabrik Netstal, CH — the 85 % yield benchmark (ZKG 1/2021)

Lime since 1900; ~90 kt/y; 1994 Maerz PFR (oil→gas 2013); **2010 stone
prep: two toothed double-roller crushers to ~100 mm; PUBLISHED rationale:
"~85 % utilisation of the limestone mined is achieved due to the toothed
double roller crushers and the very wide grain size range in the PFR
kiln"** — low-fines crushing + widened kiln envelope, by design. Both
kiln fractions (15/40 + 40/100) screened AND WASHED, fed in equal parts
via a rotating weighing bucket feeder + adjustable stone chutes for even
shaft distribution. 2020 kiln: 200 t/d PFR with patented aftercooler
(residual CO2 ~0.5 %), ignited 16 Oct 2020, on schedule through COVID;
ONE kiln now covers the whole requirement — a QUALITY-driven renewal.
Gravel co-product absorbs the non-lime fraction locally.
**Teaches**: the quantified target for WANKOE's yield problem — 85 %
deposit utilisation via gentle crushing + wide/dual kiln windows +
washing; co-product sales as the structural landfill answer.

## 4. Heidelberg Union Bridge / New Windsor, MD — scalp first, crush less

3.5-3.8 Mt/y limestone need; New Windsor reserves > 200 Mt (65+ y) 7 km
away; HAZEMAG semi-mobile plant (ordered 2015, performance test passed
March 2018): 2 500 t/h, 400 t hopper, HAF 25160 apron feeder, **HRS 2638
roller screen described as "a critical component" — pre-scalps the
minus-100 mm carrying the sticky clay and moisture AROUND the HPI 2230
impactor**; 7.2 km overland conveyor (2019) buried at 5/6 road crossings,
fully covered — the permitting artifact that made the corridor feasible;
drone-surveyed, community sites maintained, relationship positive.
FLAG: no public data on realized vs design t/h or wet-season roller-screen
behavior — operator contact only.
**Teaches**: the architecture answer for wet sticky soft rock (route wet
fines around the crusher); conveyor engineering as community engineering;
semi-mobile primary decouples reserve life from the fixed plant.

## 5. Sac-Tun (ex-Calica/Vulcan), Quintana Roo — the sovereign-risk case

Quarrying coral limestone since 1987; ~2 400 ha; own deep-water port
(Punta Venado) + 5 km conveyor, up to 12 Mt/y exported to US Gulf.
May 2022: extraction HALTED by the AMLO government (below-water-table
allegations); 14 March 2023: naval/police seizure of the terminal for
Cemex's benefit; Semarnat cenote accusations; Sept 2024 decree = protected
area; ICSID claim ~US$1.7 bn → **July 2026 ruling: ~US$15 M awarded —
under 1 % of the claim**; extraction still halted mid-2026, settlement
negotiations toward permanent closure. (MMD-sizer attribution:
industry-reported, not independently re-verified this pass — flagged.)
**Teaches**: permits granted are not permits kept; an export-only model
has no domestic constituency; arbitration is compensation of last resort,
not continuity; below-water-table extraction near karst aquifers is the
most attackable feature of a coastal carbonate operation.

## Cross-cutting due-diligence lessons for WANKOE

1. **Permit/social risk is the dominant tail risk** and scales with the
   visibility of the resource (Netstal quiet → Union Bridge proactive →
   Cockburn contested → Sac-Tun lost). Budget community/environmental
   engineering as core capex; stay above the water table; prefer land-won
   reserves not dependent on one fragile consent.
2. **Energy diversification is a survivorship trait** (Birch AD/hydrogen;
   KFN fuel switches) — design kiln-fuel/dryer-fuel optionality in.
3. **One industrial anchor + diversified local co-products beats pure
   merchant exposure** — and the local/agricultural outlets ARE the
   political constituency. Landfilling destroys margin AND creates a
   permit liability.
4. **Converged flowsheet lessons**: scalp wet fines before the crusher;
   crush gently and widen the kiln envelope (85 % utilisation proof);
   compact multi-deck sizers for fine wet cuts; wet-sand-to-kiln works
   but moves the problem to the stack.
