Scope and method

This document presents the engineering calculation of the machines of Zone 1.2, for independent validation. Method identical to the Zone-1 companion document: per machine — function, input data, constraints, first-principles model with every symbol defined, numerical application, results; then zone balance, weekly production plan, hypothesis register, references. All figures come from a deterministic flowsheet model (closed mass balances; closed circuits solved by fixed-point iteration). Revision B: the zone topology follows the process flow diagram 12-01-PFD REV18 (NACO Engineering / Carmeuse South Africa), which the project has ruled authoritative over the earlier functional specification.

Zone 1.2 in the plant

Zone 1 (companion document) produces a 0/20 mm stockpile ("KFS Fines", 10 000 t) alongside the kiln stone. Zone 1.2 reclaims this stockpile and converts it into two products: FeedLime 6/20 mm — the feedstock of Zone 1.3 (drying/milling; its firm 40 000 t/y grits contract creates a FeedLime demand of 164 880 t/y) — and AgLime 0/1.7 mm (agricultural lime, market cap 135 000 t/y). The chain (PFD tags): reclaim feeders BF.5101/BF.5102 (100 T/h) → BC.5103 → mode flap DV.5104 → screen SR.5105 (single deck, 6 mm): oversize 6/20 = FeedLime → Feed stockpile SP.5109 (6 000 t); undersize 0/6 → flap DV.5106 → BC.5110 (rated 60 T/h) → the two-stage AgLime closing: SR.5111 (1.7 mm, open circuit) → its undersize is AgLime; its oversize → CR.5113 (impact crusher) → SR.5115 (1.7 mm) → undersize joins AgLime, oversize recycles to the crusher. AgLime → BC.5116/5117 (60 T/h) → AG stockpile SP.5118 (28 000 t).

Operating modes (PFD scenario table). A (= 2A, computed here): FeedLime 6-20 + AgLime 0-1.7. B (= 2B): DV.5104 bypasses the screen — the whole reclaim goes to the Feed stockpile; exact mass identity, no machine model; forced under rain (the 1.7 mm cut collapses on wet fines) and selectable in dry weather. C (= 2C): the whole reclaim enters the AgLime loop (PFD runs this case at 60 t/h — at 100 t/h the loop rating alert fires).

Sheet 1  ·  SR.5105 — reclaim screen, single deck 6 mm

FeedLime cut · feed: BF.5101/5102 via DV.5104 · oversize 6/20 = FeedLime · undersize 0/6 → AgLime loop

1.1 Function

SR.5105 makes the FeedLime/AgLime split in one cut: everything coarser than 6 mm becomes FeedLime (Zone-1.3 feedstock), everything finer feeds the AgLime closing loop. It replaces the two-deck 15/5 mm arrangement of the earlier functional specification (superseded).

Sheet 2  ·  SR.5111 — first 1.7 mm closing screen (open circuit)

First AgLime cut · feed: 0/6 from SR.5105 · undersize = AgLime (part 1) · oversize → CR.5113

2.1 Function

SR.5111 removes, before crushing, the fraction of the 0/6 stream that is already finer than 1.7 mm — about a quarter of the loop feed. This single-pass "scalping" protects the crusher from material that needs no crushing and cuts the circulating load to nearly zero (§4.1). The screen runs in open circuit: it sees the fresh loop feed only, never a recycle.

2.2 Model and numerical application

Karra partition at \(a=1.7\) mm, \(I=0.15\) (validity note: Karra is established for a > 1 mm — the 1.7 mm cut sits near the limit; retained, flagged to the expert review). Area:

\(\displaystyle Q_b=14\times 1.7^{0.6}=19.2\ \mathrm{t/h/m^2},\qquad A=\frac{8.6\times 1.2}{19.2\times 0.347}=1.55\ \mathrm{m^2}\ \ (\text{rain }2.06)\)
(2.1)

Sheet 3  ·  CR.5113 — impact crusher (AgLime loop)

Crushes the 1.7/6 oversize to below the cut · closed loop with SR.5115 only

3.1 Function and settings

CR.5113 receives the SR.5111 oversize plus the (small) SR.5115 recycle and crushes it below 1.7 mm in essentially one pass. Settings: rotor speed \(v=40\) m/s, CSS = 1.0 mm; breakage parameters \(A=60\), \(b=0.80\) [H] (calcite drop-weight literature gives softer values — pending site tests).

3.2 Model and numerical application

\(\displaystyle E_{cs}=\frac{40^{2}}{7200}=0.2222\ \mathrm{kWh/t},\qquad t_{10}=60\left(1-e^{-0.8\times 0.2222}\right)=9.77\ \%,\qquad n=1.400\)

Sheet 4  ·  SR.5115 — second 1.7 mm closing screen (closed loop)

Checks the crusher product · undersize joins AgLime · oversize recycles to CR.5113

4.1 Function and closed-loop identity

SR.5115 verifies the crusher product: what passed 1.7 mm joins the AgLime; the residue returns to CR.5113. By mass conservation at stationarity, everything entering the loop leaves as AgLime:

\(\displaystyle \dot m_{\mathrm{AgLime}}=\dot m_{0/6}=37.78\ \mathrm{t/h\ dry\ (verified:}\ 8.61+29.18\ \mathrm{)}\)
(4.1)

The converged recycle is 0.28 t/h — a 0.9 % circulating load (the superseded single-screen loop ran at 82 %): the crusher's one-pass fineness (97.5 % < 1.5 mm) leaves the check screen almost nothing to reject. The two-stage design is markedly the better arrangement.

4.2 Model and numerical application

Karra at \(a=1.7\) mm, \(I=0.15\) (same validity note as Sheet 2); area:

\(\displaystyle A=\frac{29.2\times 1.2}{19.2\times 0.347}=5.24\ \mathrm{m^2}\ \ (\text{rain }6.99)\)
(4.2)

5  ·  Zone balance, mode B, and weekly production plan

5.1 Mass balance (mode A, dry)

\(\displaystyle \underbrace{93.00}_{\text{reclaim}}=\underbrace{55.22}_{\text{FeedLime}}+\underbrace{37.78}_{\text{AgLime}}\ \ \mathrm{t/h\ dry}\qquad(\text{relative gap}<10^{-6})\)
(5.1)

5.2 Mode B (rain, or by choice)

DV.5104 bypasses everything to the Feed stockpile: FeedLime = reclaim = 100 t/h wet, exactly (mass identity, no machine model). Mode B makes FeedLime 1.7× as fast as mode A (100 vs 59.4 t/h wet).

5.3 Annual plan (client rule c2: FeedLime demand drives; AgLime up to cap, zero surplus)

Zone-1.3 FeedLime demand: 164 880 t/y. At 59.37 t/h wet, the demand binds FIRST (before the AgLime cap): 2 777 effective hours, all in dry season, co-producing AgLime 112 822 t/y — BELOW the 135 000 t/y market cap. Zone utilization 46.3 % of the 7 500 h ceiling; FeedLime stock balanced annually; zero unsellable AgLime surplus.

Commercial arbitration pending (client). On the measured curve the c2 rule can serve the AgLime market only at ≈ 113 kt/y of its 135 kt cap: the 6 mm split simply does not produce enough 0/6. Serving the full market would over-produce FeedLime by ≈ 25 kt/y into a 6 000 t stockpile. Options under client arbitration: accept ≈ 113 kt AgLime; or find a FeedLime surplus outlet; or raise the reclaim rate/hours. This document computes the c2 rule as decided.

5.4 Weekly translation and the seasonal storage tension

Weekly ceiling: 21 × 8-h shifts = 168 h, maintenance reserve 23.8 h → net 144.2 h/week. FeedLime demand is continuous: 3 171 t/week (Zone 1.3 runs year-round).

Storage tension. The annual plan concentrates all FeedLime production in the 39 dry weeks: the seasonal swing is ≈ 13 × 3 171 ≈ 41 200 t against a 6 000 t Feed stockpile — not operable as-is. Two bounding weekly strategies: (S1) run mode B during the 13 rain weeks at the demand rate (39.6 clock h/wk): storage tension eliminated, dry weeks relax to 66.8 h — but AgLime falls to ≈ 84.6 kt/y (no AgLime in mode B). (S2) keep the annual plan and provide ≈ 41 kt of FeedLime storage (7× the PFD capacity). The choice binds the AgLime arbitration above; it belongs to the client and is NOT decided in this document.
