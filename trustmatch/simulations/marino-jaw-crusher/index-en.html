The context

Carmeuse Moha. A rainy Tuesday morning. Marino has to replace the primary jaw crusher on the main limestone line. Required capacity: 300 to 500 t/h. Limestone Mohs 3, blocks up to 1,000 mm, CE marking, 400 V / 50 Hz, container delivery. The only quote he has received, from the German market leader, is far above his budget.

Scene 01

The German hammer blow

The quote arrives in the internal mail. Backenbrecher TM1108. €142,500. Lead time 16 to 18 weeks.

Marino does the math: this is 30% above his budget envelope. And four months without a primary crusher is unthinkable for Carmeuse Moha. He knows the line cannot hold that long in degraded mode.

Scene 03

Direction Shanghai, 颚式破碎机

The contract is signed. The 30% deposit is paid. In Zenith's Shanghai workshop, the eccentric shaft, the cast steel frame, the 8-ton golden flywheel and the ABB 132 kW motor come to life under the welding torches.

Every week, Marino receives a photo and video progress report through TrustMatch. Sarah forwards the CE certificates, the factory test report and the container shipping plan. Everything is filed in Marino's dossier — in French, English and Mandarin.

Scene 04

From Shanghai to Moha in 8 weeks

Two 40' High Cube containers cross Shanghai → Antwerp in 32 days. The crusher arrives disassembled in sub-assemblies: main frame, Mn18 jaws, golden flywheel, hydraulic group, electrical cabinet pre-wired for 400 V.

The installation is completed in 5 days by the Carmeuse Moha maintenance team, remotely assisted by Zenith engineers via video and live translation. Once the concrete foundation is poured and the 80-ton crane is in place, the crusher slowly descends to its final position.

Scene 05 — The outcome

350 t/h. Week one.

Successful commissioning on the first try. From day one, the PE1000×1200 produces 350 t/h of limestone, at moderate power, without overload. The Moha line is saved.

Marino watches the machine run. The certainty of having made the right choice is on his face.
