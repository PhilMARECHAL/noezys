## 3. Flags opened at registration (before any verdict)

1. **VERTEX beneficiary**: paying a different legal entity than the
   contracting party is a classic fraud vector AND a recourse risk even
   when genuine. Requirements: the SALES CONTRACT itself must name the
   payee and the "wholly-owned subsidiary" relationship; bank details
   confirmed through an independent channel (not just this PDF); any
   later change of account treated as fraud until proven otherwise.
2. **Setting window**: no order can be signed while the machine sheet
   contradicts the 20–60 mm duty in print (Q1).
3. **100 % before shipment**: the FAT gate is real progress; the Ndola
   frame still requires retention surviving to the SITE performance
   test — to be negotiated or explicitly waived by the client.
