# WANKOE Machine Purchase — Budget Estimate (12 machines, consolidated)

**by NOEZYS** — issued 2026-08-16 (client order: budget estimate with ~10
indicative price points per machine, consolidated global budget; "do not
stop for lack of ±10 % accuracy — mobilize as many experts as needed").

**Method and honesty statement (read first).** Five parallel NOEZYS
cost-research teams swept PUBLIC price evidence on 2026-08-16: dealer and
marketplace listings (Machinio, MachineryTrader, Machineseeker, Surplus
Record, Aggregate Systems, Mascus, resale.de…), Chinese B2B platforms
(Alibaba, Made-in-China), Indian portals (IndiaMART), cost guides and
trade press. **No RFQ was sent and no vendor was contacted** (client
suspension of 2026-08-16 respected): these are NOT offers — they are
price-evidence points standing in for offers. Every figure below traces
to a real, live public source (URLs in the evidence tables); every
inference (used→new ratio, size scaling, Western-premium multiplier) is
flagged **[H]/[INF]**. Where 10 real points do not exist publicly, the
shortfall is STATED, never padded — several machine classes (Rhewum-class
precision screens, large flip-flow frames, NANOSIZ-R fine rolls) are
quote-only worldwide and have almost no public price trail.

**Accuracy class (stated, not hidden):** this is a CLASS 4/5 BUDGETARY
estimate (AACE-type), overall **−30 % / +50 %** on any single machine and
roughly **±25–30 % on the consolidated total** (single-machine errors
partially cancel). A ±10 % estimate CANNOT be produced from public data
for quote-only machine classes — it requires real offers. The instrument
for that already exists: the 12 drafted RFQs (docs/purchase/rfq/,
sending suspended). The moment the client orders sending, the returned
bids replace this document machine by machine.

**Conventions.** New machine, EX-WORKS, excluding freight, duties,
erection, civils, and spares (added separately in §3). EUR of 2026; team
FX rates 1 EUR = 1.10–1.157 USD (spread is inside the class accuracy);
escalation of older evidence +2–3 %/yr machinery PPI [H]. Asking prices
≠ transaction prices (typically settle 10–20 % lower [H]); Chinese FOB
prices exclude CE/IEC compliance and EU landing (+15–25 % [H] where a
Chinese base case is retained.
