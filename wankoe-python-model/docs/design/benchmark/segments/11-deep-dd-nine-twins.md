# Segment 11 (Wave 4) — Full Due Diligence: the Nine Capacity Twins

World benchmark wave 4, NOEZYS due-diligence reviews, 2026-08-16. Client
decision: full deep-DD on all nine twins (three trios). Every fact carries
its source in the underlying reviews; unverifiable items flagged.

## A. Structural trio

**1. Unicalce Palagiano (Taranto, IT) — EMAS IT-000807, DNV-audited.**
Stone actuals 2018-24: 255-305 kt/y (own 98.5 % CaCO3 quarry); split
"circa due terzi" kiln / one third aggregates-premix. ONE Maerz PFR kiln
(gas, comm. 1998; pet-coke line mothballed 2015 but RETAINED; biomass
co-firing PERMITTED 2024 before engineering). 2024 products: CaO 27.5 kt,
hydrate 25.1 kt, premix 98.5 kt (+ sands, putty) — **when Taranto steel
demand halved CaO (59→27 kt, 2021-23), the PREMIX TOWER built on the
sub-kiln fraction carried the site**. Energy 1.17 GJ/t product; dust 1.62 t
vs 11.9 authorized; crystalline-silica monitoring imposed after ONE ARPA
remark; rainwater recovered since 2008; quarry doubles as an official
flood-lamination basin; ZERO landfilled minerals (0/20 sold as
"Stabilizzato", 10/20 → gas-dried sand plant → premix, filter dust →
product). 45 staff. Group: Italy's #1 lime producer (€189-201 M revenue,
Carmeuse shareholder).
**2. Kalkwerk Istein (Lhoist/Rheinkalk, DE).** 650 kt/y quarried → 150 kt
lime + 170 kt stone products (~100 staff); ~72 % of rock leaves as product,
the REST IS DESIGNED INTO THE CLOSURE PLAN (old pits backfilled with 2.4 M
m³ of rail-tunnel spoil, recultivated). ETS bill ~180 kt CO2/y → €10 M
efficiency kiln (~−15 %) + public coal-exit path. **Permit masterclass**:
Kalkgraben expansion 40 → 19.5 ha voluntarily, citizen referendum WON
(~75 % for), 5-year procedure, then experience trail + company winery +
nesting walls — beside BW's flagship nature reserve (Isteiner Klotz).
Kiln inventory/fuel mix not public (flagged).
**3. Calcidrata (Alcanede, PT).** Family group (Baptista); 3 vertical
kilns, 120 kt/y lime, 2 hydration lines; **CORK DUST as kiln fuel for
20+ years** (born of no gas grid, kept as identity + cost hedge);
margin STACKING on one rock: ornamental blocks (top) / lime + hydrate /
mortars-adhesives + aglime (bottom) — "the kiln is one floor of the
house, not the house"; first ISO 9001 lime company in Portugal (2002);
in-house logistics arm. Kiln OEM/cork economics not public (flagged).

**Structural convergence:** the kiln never eats more than ⅓–½ of the
stone and NOBODY treats the rest as residue; downstream FORMULATION is
the shock absorber; fuel optionality is bought years in advance
(authorize first, engineer second); permits are won socially then
legally; dust is all-bag-filter and returned to product.

## B. Cost trio (the hard numbers)

**4. US Lime & Minerals (SEC 10-K FY2024 + FY2025).** Revenue $160.7 M
(2020) → $372.7 M (2025) **on flat tonnage** (~3.9 Mt mined) — price, not
volume; gross margin 30 % (2022) → 49 % (2025); EBITDA ≈ 47 % (2024,
derived); fuel/energy/transport = 25.9 % of revenue ≈ 47 % of cost of
revenues; capex ~9-11 % of revenue; 345 employees (~11 kt stone/emp/y);
St. Clair: 466 kt/y mined, 48-y reserves, S-K 1300 TRS; new Texas
vertical kiln ~$65 M (t/d undisclosed).
**5. Carmeuse Winchester (VA).** $45 M (2012) for 2×400 t/d QualiCal PFR
verticals replacing a rotary — **~$56 k per t/d of kiln capacity**;
30-45 % more fuel-efficient than the rotary; multi-fuel permitted, runs
gas (USGS 2016 primary); quarry life 8-15 y triggered a 415-acre
rezoning campaign; $250 k state grant.
**6. Graymont Faulkner (MB).** 117 kt/y quicklime + 80 kt/y ground
carbonate (CIM citation) — **the WANKOE-format dual-product plant proven
viable for 35+ years** on a protected Prairie transport radius; Graymont
group audited intensity 6.03 GJ/t lime, 1.26 tCO2e/t (SR2021).

**Cost benchmarks for WANKOE:** revenue potential is product-mix-driven
(USLM ≈ $82/t of stone input at full upgrading vs $10-15/t aggregate);
stress-test margins at 30 % AND 45 % gross; kiln capex 55-130 k$/t/d
(2012→2026 money); sustaining capex ~9-11 % of revenue; our paraffin
(~45 TJ/y ≈ 0.09-0.14 GJ/t) confirms a DRYING plant, not calcining
(6 GJ/t); **watch item: the electricity line (~147 k€/y) is only
coherent for crush/screen duty — fine grinding would multiply it**;
reserves must be stated auditably (< 20 y permitted = financing red
flag); price public co-funding.

## C. Climate trio (the rain playbook + the failure anatomy)

**7. Limestone Resources / ex-Ndola Lime (ZM).** Founded 1931 for the
Copperbelt; ~1 200 mm rain in ONE season (Nov-Apr). **The
recapitalization failure anatomy (sourced: ZCCM-IH ARs, Zambian
parliament 13 Feb 2020):** US$100 M contract sum hinged on ONE kiln
(VK2); **US$7.9 M paid in full without functionality verification** —
supplier then liquidated (recourse extinguished); "hot commissioning"
2016 → never optimized → 7 loss years → insolvency 2018 → 200+
retrenched 2020; refired 2022: 140 t/d of 500 nameplate; shut again May
2024 (bought competitors' lime to serve customers); ~US$120 M total
loss alleged; 2026 JV with Wonderful Group (55/45, US$30 M, phased
600 t/d). Rain never cited as a cause — equipment reliability was.
**8. Homa Lime (Koru, KE).** Family, est. 1925, ~90 t/d hydrated lime;
~1 100 mm BIMODAL rains; **wood-fired kilns on OWN eucalyptus
plantations** (fuel moisture controlled end-to-end); aglime brand
("Boresha") anchored in independent soil science (KALRO/CROPNUTS);
farm/tourism diversification smooths wet-month cash flow; bagged
hydrate = the storable buffer. A century of rain-country operation.
**9. Graymont Te Kuiti + Oparure (NZ).** ~1 500 mm YEAR-ROUND; NZ's
largest limestone quarry (1 Mt/y consented, NZ$70 M invested); gas-fired
Maerz PFR; **resource mapped into SIX chemical units incl. a named
"Aglime" unit** (steel lime < 1.7 % SiO2 by selective mining; GPR to
find karst infills); **water engineered, not dodged**: clean-water
diversion bunds, sediment ponds with flocculant, consented weekly-
sampled discharges ("could not operate without point source
discharge"), pond water reused for dust suppression (≤ 200 m³/d);
progressive backfill/rehabilitation; iwi Cultural Management Plan
funded by consent condition; Western Pit extension = ~70 y life.

**The converged rain playbook:** (1) never send weather into the kiln —
lump drains, moisture is a fines problem; (2) give every moisture state
a product — **aglime is the product whose demand the rain itself
creates**; the structural answer to "1.7 mm wet screening impossible" is
divert to aglime, don't fight the screen; (3) stockpile against the
calendar you actually have (WANKOE = Homa×Graymont hybrid: campaign
quarrying into covered draining stocks + full water engineering);
(4) engineer the water circuit like the process circuit; (5) de-risk the
fuel-moisture axis — **the paraffin dryer burns money to remove water
the flowsheet invited: shrink the drying LOAD, not the drying cost**;
(6) sell the storable buffer (bagged products); (7) rain-proof demand
(weather-independent anchors + rain-synchronized aglime).

**Ndola procurement lessons → INTO THE WANKOE RFQ FRAME:** milestone
payments against witnessed performance tests; retention money + bank
guarantees; supplier solvency verified; obligations surviving to FULL
PERFORMANCE, not delivery; commissioning budgeted as the project;
never starve the base plant for the flagship; protect the customer base
during works.

## D. The five hardest questions the twins pose to the WANKOE design

1. **Where does the 0/20 go on day one?** (product, premix feed, or a
   permitted in-pit geotechnical sink — landfilling has NO analog among
   the nine and would be indefensible under EMAS-grade disclosure).
2. **Is 85 kt/y kiln stone an anchor or an exposure?** (lime demand is
   the volatile leg — Palagiano −50 % in 2 years; what fines-based
   formulated product is our counter-cyclical floor?).
3. **What is our "cork dust"?** (the locally abundant biomass residue
   the dryer/any kiln should be specified for NOW — authorize first).
4. **What is the true kiln-feed yield of a soft porous wet stone at
   20/35 — decrepitation included?** (all nine burn hard pure stone;
   this is our genuinely unprecedented variable; only kiln trials
   answer it — joins the external-tests register).
5. **What is the 30-year social-licence plan?** (Istein proves expansion
   beside a flagship nature reserve is winnable — via a decade of
   engagement, area concessions, public-works backfill services and
   audited transparency — in the permit application, not bolted on).
