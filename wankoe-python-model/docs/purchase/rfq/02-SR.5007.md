## 1. Scope of supply

One (1) double-deck vibrating screen, apertures 35 / 20 mm, making the
KFS 20/35 product (firm 85 kt/y) and the 0/20 undersize — the largest
screen duty of the line (324.5 t/h wet feed). Complete with quick-change
modular panels on both decks, one full spare panel set per deck
(mandatory priced line), exciter condition-monitoring provision, safe
aperture-gauging access, anti-blinding panel option for the wet 20 mm
deck, commissioning supervision (mandatory priced line).

## 3. Witnessed tests

1. **KFS envelope acceptance** at 324.5 t/h wet (three-stream sieve,
   mass balance closed).
2. **Efficiency test**: measured imperfection ≤ guarantee.
3. **Capacity**: sustained worst-mode feed 2 h, no bed-depth alarm, no
   quality drift — repeated at the vendor's wet derating condition.
4. Stroke/orbit baseline on four corners + exciter vibration baseline.
5. Panel change demonstration within the vendor-stated time.

## 5. Machine-specific clauses

- Vendor verifies both areas by its OWN bed-depth / V-factor method at
  both duty points (dry 324.5 t/h wet feed at 7 % moisture; rain = same
  feed with the vendor's wet factor — engine reference 0.75 [H]) —
  value tables with declared interpolation (golden rule 3).
- Panel batches delivered with stated aperture tolerances; wear
  compound stated for 300+ t/h limestone with 5 % embedded quartz
  (§3 of the common conditions).
- Bolted (not welded) frame at fatigue hot spots or vendor fatigue
  calculation supplied.
- Spare exciter cartridge in the spares list; grease schedule stated.
