## 1. Scope of supply

One (1) smooth double-roll crusher, first crushing stage of the dry
FeedLime 6/20 (post-dryer, zone 1.3 C1 configuration) — single unit,
chain-critical. Complete with gap-drift instrumentation (position
feedback, 0.5 mm repeatability, drift alarm), overload release
(spring/hydraulic) as last line of defense behind the EM.09 metal
detection, bearing temperature + vibration monitoring wired at
purchase, spare coupling element, commissioning supervision (mandatory
priced line).

## 3. Witnessed tests

1. **Gradation acceptance = the G3 test**, witnessed, on WANKOE
   material; coefficients land data-first in the model and the engine
   replays the C1 balance BEFORE shipment is accepted.
2. **Capacity test**: 32 t/h dry sustained 2 h at gap 8.0 — no
   overload-release actuation, product P80 consistent with 7.7 mm.
3. **Downstream QC tie-in**: grits D6 envelope (< 2 mm ≤ 15 %,
   > 4 mm ≤ 5 %) and fines 0/1.5 spec at SC.B must stay reachable.
4. Bearing baselines; gap-instrumentation calibration; overload-release
   function test.

## 5. Machine-specific clauses

- Zero-speed-differential smooth rolls: pure compression is a DESIGN
  requirement (the D6 sharpness bet; differential shear raises S_att).
- Motor: worst absorbed 17.4 kW, recommended installed ≥ 22 kW [H] —
  vendor drive selection governs; installed power and roll dimensions
  close the null data slots.
- Extension provision (D1): vendor states the machine's margin above
  32 t/h (deferred 2nd-RC.2/BC.22 retrofit).
- Roll surfaces: the abrasive phase is well-rounded 200 µm quartz that
  POLISHES rather than cuts (silica REV B) — the vendor's wear figure
  must be given for that regime, not for sharp-quartz sand.
