## 1. Scope of supply

One (1) fine-cut screen, aperture 1.7 mm, open circuit, first cut of
the AgLime loop (undersize goes STRAIGHT to AgLime — ≥ 95 % < 1.7 mm,
135 kt/y commitment). Complete with quick-change fine-mesh panels + TWO
spare panel sets (fine panels are consumables — mandatory priced line),
exciter condition-monitoring provision, family cartridge with
SR.5105/SR.5115, aperture-gauging access, commissioning supervision
(mandatory priced line).

## 3. Witnessed tests

1. **AgLime spec acceptance**: undersize ≥ 95 % passing 1.7 mm at BOTH
   duty points, sieve-verified.
2. **2C endurance**: 4 h sustained at 100 t/h wet — no efficiency
   collapse, blinding, or structural alarm.
3. Efficiency/imperfection at duty ≤ guarantee (three-stream balance).
4. Exciter + stroke baselines; fine-panel quick-change demonstration.

## 4. Candidate bidders (vendor-candidates dossier)

**Binder+Co (BIVITEC ~1.5×5.0 m — best pick, sized on the bed load, not
the cut floor)**, Hein Lehmann (LIWELL LF 1.5-5.67, 8.5 m²), IFE
(TRISOMAT), JOEST (OSCILLA), Spaleck. Flip-flow is the only credible
technology class at a moist 1.7 mm cut (the project's own rain
physics — 1.7 mm wet screening is requalified as physically impossible
on conventional decks).

## 5. Machine-specific clauses

- **Bed-depth sizing at 100 t/h wet IS the real area question** — the
  catalog cut-duty floor is not the answer; the vendor delivers its
  sizing as value tables with declared interpolation (golden rule 3).
- Fine mats 1.5–2.0 mm in 0.1 steps, wear compound stated for
  quartz-bearing near-mesh fines (SR.5115's twin free-grain interface).
- Family offer with SR.5105/SR.5115 (one cartridge class) requested.
- The 60 t/h loop conveyors BC.5110/BC.5116 are being uprated in the
  same file — the vendor's screen must not be the residual bottleneck.
