# Segment 7 (Wave 2) — Asia / CIS Regional Extension

World benchmark wave 2, NOEZYS due-diligence review, 2026-08-16. 15 real
cases. Regions under-covered in wave 1: China, India, Turkey, SE Asia, CIS.

## China (~300 Mt/y lime, world #1)

- **C1 Liuzhou Iron & Steel, Guangxi — PARTIAL.** 11 Maerz PFR kilns
  (6× R4S 600 t/d added 2019); **stone window 40–80 mm**; humid
  subtropical karst; captive steel lime; on-site coal milling.
- **C2 Inner Mongolia Huaheng — PARTIAL.** 800 t/d Maerz PFR (comm. 2024,
  carbide/PVC industry); **kiln waste gas reused to DRY the limestone
  feed** — the documented model for cutting WANKOE's dryer fuel.
- **C3 Hezhou GCC cluster, Guangxi — FULL (cluster).** World's largest
  carbonate cluster (~28 Mt/y GCC in Guangxi, Hezhou ~35 %; Xinhuafa
  1.5 Mt/y, Kelong 2 Mt/y, Guiyi 3 Mt/y + 80 plants): one resource,
  full chain (slab → offcut recovery → superfine GCC → engineered stone →
  compounds → carbonate-waste recycling); policy bans raw-ore export.
  **Near-zero waste because every fraction has an adjacent buyer.**

## India

- **I1 Adani Mundra — PARTIAL.** 6× Maerz PFR R5S 800 t/d (~5 000 t/d) —
  largest greenfield lime plant ever; fired on carbide by-product gas.
- **I2 Easternbulk, Tuticorin — THIN.** Maerz E2 150 t/d (2019).
- **I3 Gotan/Nagaur-Jodhpur belt, Rajasthan — PARTIAL (cluster).**
  Quarry-level grade split: > 95 % CaCO3 to lime kilns, sub-90 % routed to
  white cement; petcoke firing under permit list.
- **I4 Katni, MP — THIN.** Historic lime cluster.
- Aglime note: monsoon climates apply aglime in the dry season as standard
  practice; India's aglime market is informal/fragmented.

## Turkey

- **T1 Kimtaş (Carmeuse), 8 sites — PARTIAL.** Turkey's largest lime
  producer; downstream construction-chemicals arm = fines valorization.
- **T2 Adaçal, Afyon — THIN.** Combined PFR lime + GCC site.
- Parget Makina (Ankara, Maerz partner) = the region's stone-prep
  engineering hub (did Abinsk).

## Southeast Asia

- **S1 Yen Bai (YBM), Vietnam — PARTIAL.** > 98.5 % white marble; ~600
  kt/y GCC across 4 factories incl. coated-powder lines, D50 to 1.5 µm;
  export cluster with Nghe An/Ha Nam. The commodity floor with Egypt.
- **S2 PMSC Bohol, Philippines — PARTIAL.** > 2 Mt/y SIZED limestone from
  a coastal carbonate in a fully wet-tropical climate, 40+ years —
  year-round sized-stone production under rain exceeding our 25 %.
- **S3 Unichamp Rio Tuba, Palawan — THIN.** US$24 M quicklime plant on a
  15-year anchor contract (Coral Bay HPAL nickel).
- **S4 PT Indo Sinar Abadi, Tuban, Indonesia — THIN.** Soft chalky
  "kapur" limestone (closest lithological analogue in SE Asia); market
  accepts CaO ≥ 85 % active lime — **spec relaxed instead of rock
  over-processed**.

## Russia / CIS

- **R1 Abinsk Electric Steel Works — PARTIAL (best window datum of wave
  2).** Maerz E2 > 160 t/d (2020): **runs TWO limestone grades — 20–40 mm
  AND 55–100 mm** — dual-window operation; 20–40 ≈ our 20/35: **our
  window is inside the proven envelope of modern two-shaft kilns.**
- **R2 Carmeuse Syktyvkar — PARTIAL.** 250 t/d kiln fed 500 t/d limestone
  at 40–80 mm; integrated crush/screen/kiln at ~our kiln-stone scale.

## Wave-2 regional synthesis

1. **Our 20/35 window is validated** (Abinsk 20–40 standard) — at the fine
   edge of world practice but inside the proven envelope; the soft-rock
   penalty is fines MASS, not kiln compatibility.
2. **Wet-tropical practice exists at scale**: accept wet stone in coarse
   circuits, fight moisture only at the fine-products step (= our
   dryer-before-fines architecture), and fuel that drying with kiln waste
   heat (Huaheng).
3. **The Hezhou clustering model**: optimize an ecosystem, not a
   flowsheet — near-zero waste by co-location.
4. **Anchor-customer lime dominates growth regions** (steel, carbide-PVC,
   HPAL nickel) — long offtakes + by-product fuels.
5. **Soft-rock producers relax spec instead of over-processing** (Tuban
   CaO 85 %; Gotan grade split).

## Top-3 additional improvements (beyond wave 1)

1. **Kiln waste-gas drying of wet feed** (Huaheng model) — duct
   calcination exhaust to the dryer/pre-dry bin.
2. **A "mini-Hezhou" valorization ring** around the 0/20 and fines
   streams — recruit co-located users; ship value-added only.
3. **Dual kiln-stone window** (Abinsk model): qualify a second coarser
   window (e.g. 35–70) alongside 20/35.

Gaps flagged, not invented: Hezhou member flowsheets, Kimtaş kiln windows,
