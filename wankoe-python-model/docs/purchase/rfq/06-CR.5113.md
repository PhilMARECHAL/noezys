## 3. Witnessed tests

1. **2C duty test**: sustained loop equilibrium 4 h — winding
   temperatures within class, absorbed power ≤ guarantee.
2. **Product acceptance**: P80 ~0.95 mm; AgLime spec at loop output.
3. **Balance acceptance** after a witnessed bar/cage change.
4. Baselines: bearing vibration/temperature + motor current signature
   at both duty points.

  architecture (the 2A/2C absorbed-power ratio is 4:1).

  insulation-test point.

  quartz (wear rank #2 of the park, silica study REV B); wear gauging
  at every campaign changeover; motor-current trend usable as wear
  proxy.

  spikes after rain weeks).
- Vendor states its breakage-parameter basis (A = 60 / b = 0.80 [H];
  no external tests launched — client decision 2026-08-16).
