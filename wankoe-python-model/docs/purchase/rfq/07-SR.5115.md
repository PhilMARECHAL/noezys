## 1. Scope of supply

One (1) fine-cut screen, aperture 1.7 mm, CLOSED loop with CR.5113
(undersize joins AgLime; oversize recycles to the crusher) — the
LARGEST screen area requirement of the line. Complete with quick-change
fine-mesh panels + TWO spare sets (mandatory priced line), exciter
condition-monitoring provision, family cartridge with SR.5105/SR.5111,
aperture-gauging access, commissioning supervision (mandatory priced
line).

## 3. Witnessed tests

1. **AgLime spec acceptance**: undersize ≥ 95 % passing 1.7 mm at the
   2C duty, sieve-verified.
2. **Loop convergence test**: stable circulating load at the 2C
   equilibrium (86.0 t/h dry through the crusher) over 4 h — no
   runaway recirculation.
3. Efficiency/imperfection at duty ≤ guarantee (three-stream balance).
4. Exciter + stroke baselines; fine-panel quick-change demonstration.
