# Segment 3 — Cement-Plant Limestone Crushing Lines Worldwide

World benchmark wave 1, NOEZYS due-diligence review, 2026-08-16. 19 real
lines (+2 minor). The world's largest installed base of limestone primary
crushing — single-product mission (0/25–0/125 mill feed), which is exactly
what makes the comparison instructive for the 4-window WANKOE line.

## Cases (condensed)

1. **Lehigh/Heidelberg Union Bridge (New Windsor), MD, USA, 2018 — FULL.**
   Sticky high-moisture limestone, lumps to 2 000 mm. Semi-mobile HAZEMAG:
   400 t hopper → apron feeder HAF 25160 (integrated spillage scraper) →
   **roller screen HRS 2638 scalps minus-100 mm BEFORE the crusher** →
   HPI 2230 impactor (HAZtronic gap). 2 500 t/h plant; 7.2 km conveyor.
   **The flagship modern pre-scalping proof.**
2. **Sac-Tun (ex-Calica/Vulcan), Quintana Roo, MX — FULL.** Coral
   limestone (porous elastic — absorbs power). **MMD twin-shaft sizer
   chosen because it breaks in tension/shear**; custom drivetrain doubled
   torque; 2 000→3 000 t/h. "May be the only machine capable of
   effectively crushing this material." Validates WANKOE's sizer choice;
   tooth/torque tuning is a real lever.
3. **Cauldon (Blue Circle→Holcim), UK — PARTIAL.** MMD 750 sizer,
   ~40-year service life, upgraded in place to 1 200 t/h.
4. **Quinn Cement, Ballyconnell, IE — PARTIAL.** MMD D4 apron feeder +
   **primary AND secondary 625 sizers**, 500 t/h, >200 rain days —
   all-sizer cascade stable on wet feed.
5. **PT Semen Bima, Java, ID — PARTIAL.** tkP TITAN double-shaft hammer
   crusher (DSHC), semi-mobile, 1 000 t/h, wet tropical limestone.
6. **Yamama Cement, KSA — PARTIAL.** 2 mobile tkP primaries 1 800 t/h
   each; fleet split by material stream.
7.–12. **TITAN DSHC fleet (vendor-rated)**: Saudi Cement Ain Dar 70D160
   1 000 t/h; Riyadh Cement 80D160 1 300 t/h walking mechanism;
   Heidelberg Burglengenfeld 80D160 1 600 t/h moist limestone/marl;
   Holcim Höver 56D75 500 t/h; Cemex San Pedro de Macorís (DO) 80D100
   1 300 t/h **coastal coralline limestone**; Qassim 60D160 900 t/h.
   DSHC = wet-sticky answer where high fines are WANTED (grate 0/25 vs
   0/80 selectable) — the anti-pattern for KFS yield.
13. **Jura Cement, Wildegg, CH — PARTIAL (strong).** One tkP PB 180/200 CR
    impactor run at **TWO product settings — 700 t/h < 80 mm and
    250 t/h < 35 mm — switched hydraulically** "without time-consuming
    modifications"; patented tramp release with defined belt stop point.
    **The multi-product-preset model for our impactor campaigns.**
14. **Cementos Progreso, GT — PARTIAL.** TWO apron feeders on one primary
    — blending/redundancy ahead of crushing.
15.–17. SPCC Bishah, Cemex Hermosillo, Loma Negra Olavarría — THIN
    (vendor-rated impactor fleet).
18. **El Sewedy, Ain Sokhna, EG — PARTIAL.** FLSmidth EV 250x300 hammer
    crusher (2.5 m boulder → 25 mm, 1:100) — horizontal feed swallows
    sticky oversize; maximal fines = opposite of KFS goal.
19. **Buzzi Unicem Guidonia, IT, 2024 — PARTIAL.** Bedeschi twin-roll
    double-stage, 750 t/h, 600→<50 mm on moist marl/limestone — low-speed
    roll crushing is being NEWLY bought in 2024 for soft moist limestone.

Minor: Volsk (RU) MMD 500/350 on additives; Belgian cement plant MMD
retrofit over an existing conveyor.

## Segment synthesis

1. **Mission asymmetry** — one product, fines free; their machine choices
   that maximize fines are anti-patterns for WANKOE; their materials-
   handling practices are the transferable layer.
2. **Pre-scalping before the primary is standard on wet sticky soft
   limestone** (Lehigh HRS; tkP roller-screen-wobbler catalog duty;
   McLanahan/Metso wobblers). At WANKOE the case is stronger: the scalped
   0/20 is SALEABLE and every bypassed tonne protects KFS yield.
3. **On porous/elastic carbonate, tension-shear (sizer) beats compression
   and impact** (Sac-Tun proof; Cauldon 40-y life; Quinn wet cascade).
4. **Feeding & tramp discipline**: big hopper → heavy apron feeder →
   choke-free feed; hydraulic tramp release with defined stop point.
5. **Multi-product from one crusher exists** — hydraulically (Jura).
6. **Weather strategy**: weather-tolerant crushers + covered prehomo
   stores; nobody dries before the raw mill.

## Top-5 improvements (this segment)

1. **Roller-screen/wobbler scalper ahead of the primary sizer** to bypass
   the natural 0/20 — the highest-leverage single change identified in
   the whole benchmark (KFS yield ↑, wear ↓, wet-fines smearing ↓).
2. Heavy apron-feeder front end + formalized tramp procedure.
3. Hydraulic remote product-window presets on the recycle impactor.
4. Sizer torque/tooth audit against porous-elastic carbonate (Sac-Tun).
5. Twin-feeder blending / covered intermediate stock for prehomogenizing
   variable dune rock.

Caveat: no public evidence exists of any cement line producing four
simultaneous sized products — that remains WANKOE-specific, which is why
the imports are practices, not flowsheets.
