# Zone 1.3 redesign — DESIGN BASIS (frozen, client-validated element by element, 2026-08-14)

The client judges zone 1.3 badly dimensioned (machine choice and
performance) and ordered a from-scratch redesign. Every element below was
validated individually by the client.

| # | Item | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Duty | ONE machine park for BOTH regimes: 40 000 t/y grits (initial) and 80 000 t/y (target) — no equipment added or removed between regimes; only feed rates, settings and hours differ. Sizing case = 80 000 t/y |
| D2 | Fixed equipment | Dryer DY.03 ACQUIRED: 30 t/h at outlet (= 32.1 t/h wet feed, 7 % -> 0.5 %), the single drying line for both regimes. Everything else free (screening, milling, classification, conveying) |
| D3 | Fines constraint | FIRM: fines/grits ratio <= 1.25 (80 kt fits the single dryer inside the hours ceiling) · OPTIMIZATION OBJECTIVE: ratio <= 1.0 (80 kt at ~89 % utilization, real margin). Capacity arithmetic: 80 000 t / 6 000 eff h = 13.33 t/h grits from <= 30 t/h dried product -> grits share >= 44 % (today: 26 %) |
| D4 | Design feed | Sized on the FeedLime 6/20 from the CURRENT measured chain; every candidate ALSO verified on the quarry-target variant (41 % < 20 mm at zone-1 inlet). Zone 1.3 is analyzed as a function of the feed quality at ZONE 1's inlet, propagated by the engine through zones 1/1.2 |
| D5 | Shift frame | 3 x 8 h continuous, 7 500 h/y ceiling, availability 80 % (6 000 eff h/y) |
| D6 | Grits quality | 2-4 mm: oversize > 4 mm <= 5 % · undersize < 2 mm <= 15 % · moisture 0.5 % — both thresholds as adjustable parameters; no contractual spec existed, criteria adopted by the client 2026-08-14 |
| D7 | Co-products | Fines 0/1.5 sellable (60 kt market + AgLime-channel compatible); UltraFin extraction kept as an OPTION (no tonnage requirement); product moisture 0.5 % |
| D8 | Commercial frame | Zero unsellable product (fines redirect rules), upstream 0/20 excess = landfill loss to avoid by construction, absolute priority to the firm contracts (KFS 85 kt, grits 40 -> 80 kt). Candidates judged on the FULL line balance |

Panel mission (on explicit client GO): (1) instructed critique of the
existing circuit — why it does 2.7 when 1.25 is required (ML.26 product
spread, SN.21 cuts, closed-loop overgrinding); (2) candidate circuits
sized to D1-D8; (3) engine modelling of the best candidates on both feed
qualities; (4) adversarial verification; (5) design note for client
arbitration.
