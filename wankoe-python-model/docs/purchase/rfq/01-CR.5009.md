## 1. Scope of supply

One (1) twin-shaft toothed SIZER, primary crushing of the WANKOE
zone-1.1 pivot feed, complete with: vendor-standard twin-drive package
(2 × ~110 kW class with soft starters), internal hydraulic overload
relief, outdoor weather execution (IP-rated drives, covered lubrication,
walkway protection), bearing temperature probes wired to trend alarms,
gap indication repeatable to the 5 mm step, jam-clearing access and
procedure, initial critical spares kit (one full tooth-segment set + one
shaft — mandatory priced line), commissioning supervision (mandatory
priced line). Greenfield installation — the vendor optimizes feed height
and discharge (no as-built envelope constraint).

Also in the RFQ perimeter (may be quoted by the same or a separate
bidder): metal detector + overband magnet on the feed belt, detection
threshold agreed with the sizer vendor (client decision Q10).

## 3. Witnessed tests

1. **Gradation acceptance** on WANKOE feed (or witnessed equivalent):
   product curve at g = 60 within the vendor table tolerance; P80
   consistent with 42.7 mm (engine reference).
2. **Capacity test**: 250 t/h wet sustained 2 h at g = 60, no relief
   actuation on in-spec feed.
3. **Power verification**: absorbed at duty ≤ guarantee (engine
   reference 96.6 kW).
4. Bearing temperature and vibration baselines at commissioning.
5. Belt-cut PSD at the outlet accepted by the vendor as the plant's
   wear-trend/acceptance measurement basis (the ONLY plant measurement
   point).

## 5. Machine-specific clauses

- The offer states the smallest standard drive package covering the
  duty (installed 220 kW class ≫ absorbed 96.6 kW) — no minimum-drive
  engineering requested.
- Bolt-on replaceable tooth SEGMENTS (not welded), profile gauging
  templates supplied; segment metallurgy stated for the quartz-bearing
  limestone duty.
- Vendor states its gradation table at gaps 20–60 mm on WANKOE stone
  (value table, declared interpolation — golden rule 3) and its power
  guarantee basis vs Wi 12.54 kWh/t [ref.] (site Bond test not yet run).
- 7–12 % feed moisture handling stated (outdoor stocks, 25 % rain time).
