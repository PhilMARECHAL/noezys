## 2. The consolidated improvement portfolio (ranked, evidence-backed)

**A. Structural / commercial (largest levers)**
1. **Renegotiate the KFS window** — widen (20/40–20/50) and/or a second
   caliber (35/60 or 10/20 to a Finelime/rotary customer). Evidence: D1
   fleet; Netstal 85 % utilisation. Expected: KFS yield 24.9 → 35–50 %,
   landfill and impactor recycle collapse. *Client action: one
   negotiation.*
2. **Fines ladder instead of landfill** — in order (segment 9 verdicts):
   cement raw (both streams, haul-gated) → sinter flux (quartz passes) →
   FGD crushed stone (conditional) → granulated aglime (5 % SiO2
   commercially normal) → EN 13043 filler (needs drying/milling) →
   roadbase backstop. Honest fails: rock dust, BOF flux, FGD flour.
3. **The 65 µm purification play** (GCC dossier): assay first (8 numbers,
   incl. brightness — eolianites fail on yellowness, not quartz), sell
   fines at feed/agri grade NOW, scale the CUT (12–18 kt/y quartz-free
   intermediate), phase-gate a mini-GCC mill on offtakes, partner with a
   carbonate house (Provençale; Suomen Karbonaatti JV model).
4. **FeedLime = fraction screening + blending, GMP+/FEMAS certified**
   (D6); market the 6/20 to Finelime/PFR kiln operators as premium
   small-stone feed (Calidra).

**B. Flowsheet (engineering)**
5. **Pre-scalp the natural 0/20 ahead of the primary sizer**
   (roller-screen/wobbler — Lehigh Union Bridge "critical component"):
   KFS yield ↑, sizer wear ↓, wet-fines smearing ↓, scalped product
   saleable.
6. **Wet-capable screening for the volume cuts** (roller screens for
   35/20 and 6 mm; flip-flow/cage-mill options for AgLime; damp-AgLime
   second tier) — converts most of the 25 % rain time into production.
7. **Moisture before fuel**: covered surge/kiln-stone storage, drainage
   windrows, mechanical dewatering where slurried, **kiln/dryer
   waste-heat recovery** (Huaheng/Dankalk models) — attacks the paraffin
   bill (1.3 ML/y).
8. **Gentle-crushing audit of the KFS loop** (Netstal doctrine): study a
   roll/sizer secondary vs the CR.5011 impactor recycle — the impactor
   is a fines generator feeding our own landfill. (CR.5011 is
   purchase-framed already — this is a STUDY item before contract.)
9. **20 mm guard screen at KFS load-out** (Réty screen-twice doctrine) —
   soft porous stone degrades in stockpiles.
10. **Surface-miner trial** (IFCEM/Wirtgen, proven at UCS 20–70): one
    machine = ripper + CR.5009 + selective quartz-bench mining. A
    quarry-works alternative to evaluate against the sizer purchase.

**C. Quality & control**
11. **Cross-belt analyzer + quarry silica block model** (Graymont PA/NZ):
    protect the 85 kt/y KFS contract and steer quartz at the face; XRT
    sorting on the watchlist if geology shows discrete silica lenses
    (Faxe).

**D. Due-diligence & resilience (from the deep dives)**
12. **Community/environmental engineering as core capex** (enclosure,
    public monitoring, buried/covered conveyors), stay above the water
    table, hold a second politically clean reserve (Dongara lesson);
    anchor offtake + local agricultural constituency (Sac-Tun's fatal
    lack); dryer/kiln fuel optionality (Birch AD→hydrogen path).

## 3. What the benchmark does NOT change

The four-window mission is confirmed UNIQUE (D10) — no whole-flowsheet
copy exists. The machine selections of the purchase file survive the
benchmark: the sizer class is validated on porous carbonate (Sac-Tun),
the fine screens' flip-flow choice matches world practice, and the 20/35
window itself is inside the proven envelope of modern two-shaft kilns
(Abinsk 20–40). What the benchmark challenges is the FLOWSHEET AROUND
the machines: the single window, the landfill line, the rain bypass, the
precision-gap FeedLime concept, and the unpriced 65 µm purification
asset.
