# Segment 1 — Lime & Limestone Plants: Americas / Asia / Oceania / MEA

World benchmark wave 1, NOEZYS due-diligence review, 2026-08-16. 20 real
cases (1 candidate excluded as unverifiable). Depth flags FULL/PARTIAL/THIN
per case; sources at foot. Baseline = the WANKOE line as of commit df8e08a.

## Closest rock analogues

1. **Cockburn Cement — Munster (Woodman Point), WA, Australia — FULL.**
   Marine shellsand ~92 % CaCO3 dredged offshore (Tamala eolianite is the
   onshore equivalent — WANKOE's rock family). NO crushing plant at all:
   wet sand → drained stockpiles → two ~450 kt/y rotary lime kilns whose
   front end dries. Slurry pumping 6 km. Quicklime for alumina.
   *vs WANKOE*: embraces the fine wet nature instead of making lump; zero
   landfill concept; single-product simplicity. *Improvement*: if any kiln
   partner accepts fine feed, the 0/20 surplus becomes feedstock; wet
   handling of soft carbonate proven at 900 kt/y scale.
2. **Aglime of Australia — Lancelin limesand, WA — PARTIAL.** Coastal
   aeolian lime sand dunes free-dug, screening only, no crushing, no
   drying; ~10–12-week Feb–Apr sales campaign. *vs WANKOE*: aglime by
   deposit selection; mature campaign logistics; sells moist.
3. **Carmeuse/Majan — Salalah, Oman + Dhofar Limestone Mughsail — THIN.**
   ~750 kt/y lime plan, port-at-fence export model; 4.2 Mt/y crushed-stone
   quarry. *Improvement*: 0/20 as export/construction commodity; covered
   kiln-stone stocks in monsoon (khareef) practice.
4. **Graymont (McDonald's Lime) — Oparure/Otorohanga, NZ — PARTIAL, high
   silica relevance.** >95 % CaCO3; wet Waikato climate; steel-grade lime
   < 1.7 % SiO2 achieved by **silica mapping + selective mining at the
   face**, not process removal. Fine products made at the quarry plant.
   *Improvement*: silica block model + face steering for WANKOE.

## North America process-discipline benchmarks

5. **Graymont Cricket Mountain, UT — PARTIAL.** 5 kilns ~900 kt/y built
   incrementally 1980–2005+; desert; pulverized-limestone co-products
   absorb fines. *Improvement*: PLS mill route for surplus 0/20.
6. **Graymont Pleasant Gap, PA — FULL.** Deep underground mine; **MMD
   sizers underground + triple-deck screens + Thermo cross-belt PGNAA
   analyzer**; TWO kiln-stone windows (≈70 mm rotary / ≈100 mm shaft);
   waste-heat 3.5 MW turbine. *Improvement*: cross-belt analyzer ahead of
   the KFS bin; negotiate a second kiln window.
7. **Mississippi Lime, Ste. Genevieve, MO — FULL.** Largest lime facility
   in the Americas; underground ultra-pure; 11 rotary + 4 Maerz shaft
   kilns, hydrators, **two PCC units + GCC lines** — the full fines value
   ladder on one site. *Improvement*: micronized/GCC step = highest-value
   fines route.
8. **Carmeuse Maysville + Black River, KY — PARTIAL.** Underground, barge
   logistics; ~3 Mt/y stone, 5 kilns; **2:1 stone-to-lime ratio openly
   managed — surplus pre-sold as crushed stone**. *Improvement*: plan the
   stone balance, pre-sell the arithmetic surplus.
9. **Lhoist Clifton, TX — PARTIAL.** 50+ years; staged kiln additions;
   2026 doubling. *Improvement*: design zone-1.1 handling for a future
   second offtake.
10. **US Lime & Minerals — Texas Lime, Cleburne — FULL (mine).** >96 %
    CaCO3 cut-off grade control; ~1.4 Mt/y; sells quicklime, hydrate,
    **lime kiln dust, PLS/GCC and aglime** — even dust is a product.
11. **Linwood Mining, Davenport, IA — PARTIAL.** Open pit converted to
    underground to protect purity and de-weather; 4 kilns; post-kiln lime
    screening tower. *Improvement*: test WANKOE 20/35 for decrepitation in
    calcination early (soft porous stone degrades kiln screening balance).
12. **Pete Lien & Sons, Rapid City, SD — PARTIAL.** Regional multi-product
    quarry-lime hybrid at WANKOE-like scale; bagged retail as margin
    buffer. *Improvement*: small bagging line for AgLime/UltraFin.
13. **Greer Lime, Riverton, WV — PARTIAL.** Dedicated limestone grinding
    system beside kilns; portable crushing unit. *Improvement*: rent mobile
    crushing/screening for dry-season campaigns.

## Latin America

14. **Grupo Calidra, San Luis Potosí, MX — PARTIAL.** 31+ Maerz PFR kilns,
    100 % PFR fleet; **Maerz Finelime kiln bought specifically to burn the
    SMALL-stone fraction (~15–40 mm and finer)**. *Key improvement*: market
    WANKOE 6/20 explicitly to Finelime/PFR operators — premium feed, not
    byproduct.
15. **ICAL, São José da Lapa, MG, Brazil — THIN** (+ Votorantim aglime
    network, +700 kt/y Nobres expansion). Aglime as a cement-quarry
    co-product; dry-season dispatch to Cerrado; roofed sheds.
16. **Calcesur, Juliaca, Peru — PARTIAL.** 125 kt→1.0 Mt/y quicklime for
    Andean mines; one anchor market de-risks the plant. *Improvement*:
    pursue one anchor industrial offtaker for the kiln-stone line.

## Asia / Oceania / Africa

17. **Chememan, Kaeng Khoi, Thailand — PARTIAL** (+ Golden Lime/Carmeuse
    Saraburi). Monsoon practice: **dry-season crush-ahead, covered buffer,
    burn from stock in the wet**. *Improvement*: the template for WANKOE's
    25 % rain time.
18. **Yoshizawa Lime, Kuzuu, Japan — THIN.** 4 rotary kilns + **1
    fluidized-bed kiln burning FINE limestone**; blending facility.
    *Improvement*: FBC calcination of 0/2 fines is proven.
19. **KP Lime (ex-PPC) Lime Acres + Idwala Danielskuil, South Africa —
    PARTIAL.** ~900 kt/y class; rotary + shaft mixed fleets consuming two
    stone windows.
20. **Adbri Angaston/Penrice, SA — THIN.** Grade-zoned quarry: high-grade
    core to kiln, halo to aglime/aggregate.

Excluded: "Sagar" (India) — not verifiable as a lime plant (cement only).

## Segment synthesis

1. **Nobody landfills carbonate fines** — every operator has a fines
   ladder (aglime → PLS → LKD → GCC/PCC → or burns fines directly).
2. **Soft/coastal operators avoid fighting the rock** (burn/sell as sand;
   or de-weather by going underground/desert).
3. **Kiln-stone windows are negotiable and plural** (≥2 windows at
   Graymont PA, Idwala, Calidra).
4. **Campaigning on weather is normal** (WA Feb–Apr; Thai crush-ahead;
   Brazilian dry-season aglime).
5. **Embedded silica is managed at the face** (NZ silica mapping).

## Top-5 improvements for WANKOE (this segment)

1. Kill the landfill via a fines ladder (PLS/FGD, LKD-style, GCC later).
2. Market FeedLime 6/20 as Finelime/PFR premium small-stone feed; open a
   second kiln window.
3. Dry-season crush-ahead + covered kiln-stone/AgLime buffers.
4. Cross-belt analyzer + quarry silica block model on the KFS chain.
5. Evaluate fines calcination as the endgame (Cockburn/FBC models).
